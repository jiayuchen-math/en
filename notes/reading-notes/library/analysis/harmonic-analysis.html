Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Understand Fourier series and transforms and their convergence before specialized decompositions. Measure and norm estimates become central in advanced reading.

Branches to choose from

  • Classical Fourier theory
  • maximal / singular integral operators
  • group harmonic analysis
  • wavelets / frames
  • microlocal and PDE methods

How to continue

Move toward Littlewood–Paley / function-space tools for nonlinear PDE, Fourier integral operators for microlocal geometry, or time-frequency methods for wavelets. Their preparatory requirements differ.
