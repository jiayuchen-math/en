Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Build sigma-algebras, measures and Lebesgue integration before using convergence theorems. Always check measurability and integrability.

Branches to choose from

  • Measure / integration foundations
  • product measures
  • differentiation
  • martingales
  • geometric measure theory

How to continue

Probability texts use measure theory but have additional stochastic goals. Currents and rectifiability are a separate geometric-analysis branch requiring substantial preparation.
