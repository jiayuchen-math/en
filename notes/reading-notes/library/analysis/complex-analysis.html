Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Practice holomorphic functions, contour integrals and analytic continuation. Geometric intuition and rigorous convergence arguments should develop together.

Branches to choose from

  • One complex variable
  • meromorphic functions
  • elliptic / modular functions
  • Riemann surfaces
  • several complex variables

How to continue

Choose algebraic functions and Riemann surfaces for geometry, modular functions for arithmetic, or several variables for complex geometry. These are continuations, not chapters to read in a mandatory order.
