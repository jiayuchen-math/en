Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Learn Banach and Hilbert spaces, bounded maps, duality and the main completeness arguments. Compute examples of weak convergence.

Branches to choose from

  • Normed spaces and operators
  • weak / spectral methods
  • semigroups
  • variational analysis
  • nonlinear / computational applications

How to continue

For PDE, prioritize weak compactness and variational tools; for operator theory, develop spectral methods. Optimization and numerical variational notes are explicitly applied branches.
