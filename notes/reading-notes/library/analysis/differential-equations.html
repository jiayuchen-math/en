微分方程：找到公式之后还要检查什么？

独立中文导读 · 非逐句全文翻译 · 修订 2026-10-18

写出一个函数并验证它满足方程，是学习微分方程的开始，不是结束。还要问：它是否满足初值？定义到哪里？是否还有别的解？即使没有显式公式，能否判断增长、稳定或长期行为？

先把解放回方程里

推荐 Lebl 的 Notes on Diffy Qs。1.3–1.4 看一阶方法，3.3–3.4 与 3.8 看线性系统和矩阵指数，4.1–4.4 处理傅里叶边界展开，再到 4.6–4.7 的热方程与波方程。先用熟悉的微积分和线性代数计算，不要把“能分离变量”误当作所有方程都能求解。

如果解法熟练却不知道唯一性从哪来，可另读 Teschl 的不动点与存在唯一性部分，再接初值依赖与解的延拓。条件要逐项检查：右端连续与对状态变量局部 Lipschitz 并非同义。研究 y′=∣y∣y'=\sqrt{|y|}y′=∣y∣​ 的零初值问题，可以看见“已经找到一个解”为什么不证明它是唯一解。

从一个初值问题到一条热流

先解 y′+2y=e−ty'+2y=e^{-t}y′+2y=e−t、y(0)=0y(0)=0y(0)=0，然后代回原式与初值核对。再在 0<x<π0<x<\pi0<x<π 上考虑零端点热方程，初态为 sin⁡x+3sin⁡2x\sin x+3\sin2xsinx+3sin2x：分别检查每个空间模式的衰减率，最后同时验证方程、边界和初始条件。只把三个条件中的一个算对，不能叫解完这个问题。

Lebl 的书有习题与选题答案，编号需和实际版本配对；不能把选题答案部分说成每题的完整解答。若一阶公式已很熟，可以略读重复计算，把时间放在系统、初值条件与定性判断上。

常微分方程的初值理论、动力系统的长期行为、PDE 的弱解与正则性是不同的后续入口。分离变量给出的是一条有用的显式路线；走向一般 PDE 时，还需要测度、函数空间与估计，不是再增加几种求原函数的技巧。

Studying Differential Equations & PDEs

Start here · Choose a text · Practice · Check your understanding · Continue · Full reading list

The equation, initial data and boundary conditions together determine the problem. Lebl gives an entry through first-order methods, systems and separated heat and wave equations. Dyatlov studies distributions and elliptic regularity at graduate level. Choose the computational route to learn what solutions and boundary conditions do; choose the analytic route only when measure, functional and Fourier analysis are available as working tools.

What to work on

  • Solve separable and first-order linear initial-value problems.
  • Solve a constant-coefficient system using eigenvalues or matrix exponentials.
  • Derive separated solutions of the heat and wave equations with boundary conditions.
  • Check that a proposed solution satisfies both the equation and the initial or boundary data.

A starting text

Notes on Diffy Qs: Differential Equations for Engineers — Jiří Lebl. Read 1.3-1.4 for first-order methods, 3.3-3.4 for linear systems and 3.8 for matrix exponentials, and 4.1-4.4 for Fourier boundary expansions. Continue with 4.6-4.7 for the heat and wave equations; 4.8 supplies d'Alembert's alternative on the line.

Choosing what to read

Verify a proposed solution by substitution and check its data separately. For a linear system, compare a matrix exponential with the geometry of its phase portrait. In separation of variables, derive the boundary eigenfunctions before writing a series and explain what convergence would justify the final substitution. A formal solution formula does not establish existence or uniqueness. When you need weak derivatives, pause to learn test functions and distributions before taking on elliptic regularity.
