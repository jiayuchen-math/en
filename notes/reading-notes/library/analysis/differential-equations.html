Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Begin with ODE examples and elementary PDE models, then learn distributions, Sobolev spaces and weak formulations.

Branches to choose from

  • ODE and qualitative theory
  • elliptic / variational PDE
  • wave and dispersive equations
  • stochastic PDE
  • numerical / mechanics applications

How to continue

For pure PDE, study existence, uniqueness and regularity before a chosen research problem. Finite elements and elasticity are mathematical applications, not substitutes for every analytic foundation.
