Studying Real Analysis

What to work on

  • Write epsilon proofs for limits of sequences.
  • Use completeness to prove convergence of monotone bounded or Cauchy sequences.
  • Distinguish continuity from uniform continuity by examples.
  • Test uniform convergence before interchanging limits, integrals or derivatives.

A starting text

Basic Analysis I: Introduction to Real Analysis, Volume I — Jiří Lebl. Read sections 2.1-2.4 on sequences and completeness, 3.2-3.4 on continuity, and 6.1-6.2 on uniform convergence and interchange of limits. Review 1.2 on the real numbers if the least-upper-bound property is unfamiliar.

Practice

Sequence, continuity and convergence exercises. Exercises embedded in sections 2.1-2.4, 3.4 and 6.1-6.2; include proof and counterexample questions.. Exercises provided; public solutions not verified.
