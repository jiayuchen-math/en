Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Learn completeness, limits and continuity through proofs and counterexamples. Distinguish pointwise and uniform statements carefully.

Branches to choose from

  • Real / metric foundations
  • sequences and series
  • differentiation / integration
  • several variables
  • potential and approximation theory

How to continue

Move to measure theory for integration, functional analysis for spaces and operators, or specialized potential / approximation questions. Historical expositions should be compared with modern notation.
