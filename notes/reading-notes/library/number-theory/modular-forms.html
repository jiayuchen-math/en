Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Learn the upper half-plane, transformation laws and Fourier expansions through examples. Complex analysis is essential.

Branches to choose from

  • Classical modular forms
  • Hecke / computational methods
  • elliptic curves
  • half-integral weights
  • Siegel / automorphic forms

How to continue

Higher genus, half-integral weight and automorphic representations are different branches. Arithmetic and representation-theoretic prerequisites depend on the chosen route.
