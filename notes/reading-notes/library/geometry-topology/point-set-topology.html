Studying Point-Set Topology

What to work on

  • Prove continuity using inverse images of open sets.
  • Distinguish connectedness, compactness and separation assumptions in examples.
  • Use bases, subspaces and product topologies.
  • Verify the defining property of a quotient topology.

A starting text

Topology Without Tears — Sidney A. Morris. Read 1.1-1.3 on topologies, 2.2-2.3 on bases, 3.1 and 3.3 on closure and connectedness, 4.1 and 5.1 on subspaces and continuity, 7.1-7.2 on compactness, 8.1-8.2 on products, and 11.1 on quotient spaces.

Practice

Topology exercises and worked examples. Exercise sets 3.3, 5.1, 7.1-7.2, 8.1 and 11.1. In Exercises 11.1, questions 1-4 check the quotient-map definition and composition.. Exercises and worked examples are public. On the title page the author explicitly states that he does not provide solutions to the exercises.

Try these before moving on

  • Prove that a continuous bijection from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism. Give a continuous bijection that fails to be a homeomorphism when these hypotheses are dropped.
  • Show that identifying 0 and 1 in [0,1], with the quotient topology, produces a space homeomorphic to the unit circle; justify continuity of the induced map.

Already comfortable with this? Skip the definitions you can use correctly in proofs. Skip this selection only if both checks, a product-topology argument, and the distinction between connectedness and path connectedness are secure.
