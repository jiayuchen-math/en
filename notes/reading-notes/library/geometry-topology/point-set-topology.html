Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Work with open sets, continuity, products, quotients, compactness and connectedness in examples. Counterexamples clarify missing assumptions.

Branches to choose from

  • Metric spaces
  • separation and convergence
  • compactness
  • quotient / product constructions
  • manifold / polyhedral bridges

How to continue

Choose manifold topology or algebraic topology after the common foundation. A long general-topology reference is for selected questions, not a requirement to memorize every space.
