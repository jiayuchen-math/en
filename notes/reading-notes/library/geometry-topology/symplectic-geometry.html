Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Start with differential forms, Darboux-type local structure and Hamiltonian flows. Then study global examples and reduction.

Branches to choose from

  • Symplectic / Hamiltonian foundations
  • contact geometry
  • moment maps
  • Floer theory / Fukaya categories
  • virtual structures

How to continue

Fukaya categories and virtual-chain frameworks are technical research material. Study holomorphic curves, Floer methods and A-infinity algebra as needed; competing virtual frameworks are not mandatory consecutive steps.
