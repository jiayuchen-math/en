Differential & Riemannian Geometry

Prerequisite path Preparation & skip guidance · Linear Algebra · Calculus · Point-Set Topology

Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Develop smooth manifolds, tangent bundles, differential forms and connections before reading curvature-driven arguments.

Branches to choose from

  • Smooth / Riemannian geometry
  • geometric analysis
  • Lie / homogeneous geometry
  • complex / toric geometry
  • derived smooth geometry

How to continue

Choose curvature and PDE for geometric analysis, Lie methods for symmetry, or complex / derived directions with their algebraic prerequisites. Toric notes are an algebraic-geometric bridge, not a replacement for manifold foundations.
