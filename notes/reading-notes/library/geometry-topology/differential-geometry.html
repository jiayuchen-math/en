What Is Differential Geometry? Curves and Surfaces

Anton Petrunin and Sergio Zamora Barrera · Lecture text · Introductory

A visual, problem-centered approach to classical curves and surfaces. Emphasizes essential techniques; the authors describe its presentation as elementary and 'virtually rigorous'.

Preparation: Multivariable calculus; linear algebra.

Lectures on Spaces of Nonpositive Curvature

Access: Direct open PDF

The linked author, university or academic-archive PDF can be opened without institutional login. Availability can change; downloading does not grant redistribution rights.

Werner Ballmann; appendix by Misha Brin · Monograph / author text · Advanced

Develops nonpositive curvature, boundaries at infinity and rank rigidity, with an appendix on geodesic-flow ergodicity. A specialized continuation, not a general first Riemannian geometry textbook.

Preparation: Riemannian geometry; topology; some dynamical systems.
