Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Build and compute chain complexes, then practice exact sequences, resolutions, Ext and Tor. Do not start with derived notation alone.

Branches to choose from

  • Derived functors
  • spectral sequences
  • derived categories
  • group cohomology
  • cyclic homology / K-theory

How to continue

Choose a first-course reference before higher or derived methods. Computational homology, group cohomology and algebraic K-theory are related but not interchangeable reading goals.
