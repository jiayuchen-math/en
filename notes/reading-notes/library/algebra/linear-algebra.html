Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Learn vector spaces and linear maps alongside matrix calculations. Prove rank–nullity and understand how a change of basis changes a matrix.

Branches to choose from

  • Vector-space foundations
  • canonical forms
  • matrix inequalities
  • numerical decompositions
  • module viewpoint

How to continue

Choose matrix analysis for spectral inequalities, numerical algebra for stable computation, or structural algebra for modules. These branches share foundations but have different goals.

Access: Direct open PDF

Full 116-page survey opened on coauthor Tropp's Caltech website.

A substantial survey connecting random embeddings and matrix sampling to least squares, low-rank approximation and linear solvers.

Use: Bridge a numerical linear algebra course to randomized algorithms and their analysis.
