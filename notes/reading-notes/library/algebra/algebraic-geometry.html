Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Learn commutative rings and geometric examples, then schemes, sheaves and cohomology. There is no need to complete every frontier text in this list.

Branches to choose from

  • Schemes / sheaves
  • stacks
  • etale and p-adic methods
  • DAG / SAG
  • motives and six operations

How to continue

Choose a frontier around a question: arithmetic cohomology, derived geometry or moduli theory. A foundational research paper and an unfinished book draft must not be mistaken for an introductory textbook.
