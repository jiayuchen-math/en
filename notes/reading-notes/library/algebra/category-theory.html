Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Verify functors, natural transformations, universal properties and adjunctions in examples from sets, modules and topology.

Branches to choose from

  • Ordinary categories
  • enriched categories
  • categorical logic / topoi
  • derived sheaves
  • infinity categories

How to continue

A topos or infinity-category reference is not a first course. Use ordinary categories first, then select the additional algebra, topology or logic required by your chosen higher framework.
