Category Theory

范畴论：先找到同一种构造

独立中文导读 · 非逐句全文翻译 · 修订 2026-10-18

集合的笛卡尔积、向量空间的积，看起来由不同元素组成，却回答着同一种映射问题：给出到两个对象的映射，能否唯一地合成一个到新对象的映射？范畴论从这种重复出现的构造起步，比从一长串新术语起步更容易落地。

先在两个熟悉的范畴里试

推荐 Riehl 的 Category Theory in Context。已经知道范畴公理的读者，可从 1.3–1.4 的函子与自然性开始，再读 2.1–2.3 的可表函子、Yoneda 与泛性质，3.1–3.2 的极限，最后看 4.1–4.2 的伴随。最好带着线性代数或群、环的例子来；只在集合范畴里看见一件事，未必看得出它在哪些范畴中失败。

把积的泛性质写成对每个对象 XXX 都成立的映射对应，并检查与 XXX 的映射复合时是否相容。“两个 Hom 集合碰巧等势”比自然对应弱得多。再比较遗忘群结构的函子与取对偶的构造，注意后者会反转箭头方向。

不把语言学成口号

试着用泛性质证明两个积对象之间存在唯一的、与投影相容的同构；随后在自由群与集合的伴随中，写出生成元的集合映射怎样唯一延拓为群同态。前一题检查唯一性，后一题检查一个抽象对应究竟怎样把映射变来变去。

书中穿插着练习，公开完整解答未核实。每遇到一个自然性断言，至少画一次交换方块，追踪一个具体元素或复合；若主要障碍是基本同态与商的例子，先回代数，而不是读更多范畴定义。

普通范畴论可以边使用边补，不必把整本书变成所有数学的共同先修课。之后接同调代数或层的语言都很自然；若目标是无穷范畴与同伦论，还需要理解同伦与高阶相容，不能把普通范畴中的等式机械换成“差不多相等”就算完成推广。

Studying Category Theory

Start here · Choose a text · Practice · Check your understanding · Continue · Full reading list

Category theory becomes usable when a definition summarizes constructions you can already perform. Products, quotient maps and free objects give functors and universal properties something to act on. Riehl develops the language with mathematical context and exercises; Mac Lane gives a compact graduate account extending through adjunctions and monads. Begin with a small collection of algebraic examples and enlarge it as the abstractions demand.

What to work on

  • Check functoriality and naturality in examples of sets or modules.
  • Identify a representing object from a universal property.
  • Construct products, equalizers and their universal cones.
  • Describe an adjunction by a natural hom-set bijection and its unit and counit.

A starting text

Category Theory in Context — Emily Riehl. Read sections 1.3-1.4 on functoriality and naturality; 2.1-2.3 on representability and Yoneda; 3.1-3.2 on limits in categories and sets; and 4.1-4.2 on adjunctions and universal arrows. Review 1.1 only if category axioms are unfamiliar.

Choosing what to read

For every functor, state its action on both objects and arrows and check composition. For every natural transformation, draw a square with a specific map instead of saying that compatibility is obvious. Derive a product or free-object construction from its universal property, then test it in sets and modules. Approach Yoneda by writing out the correspondence in a concrete representable example. An unfamiliar higher-category reference can wait until these ordinary categorical calculations are dependable.

Try these before moving on

Try a proof or calculation before opening the hint. These solutions address the checks below, not the exercises in the linked textbooks.

  1. Prove the universal property of the set-theoretic product X times Y and show that any two products of X and Y are uniquely isomorphic as cones.

    A hint

    The projections force the two coordinates of the mediating map. Then apply uniqueness twice to the composites between two proposed products.

    Work through the solution

    For sets X,YX,YX,Y, let P=X×YP=X\times YP=X×Y with coordinate projections pX,pYp_X,p_YpX​,pY​. For any set ZZZ and maps f:Z→Xf:Z\to Xf:Z→X, g:Z→Yg:Z\to Yg:Z→Y, define h(z)=(f(z),g(z))h(z)=(f(z),g(z))h(z)=(f(z),g(z)). Then pXh=fp_Xh=fpX​h=f and pYh=gp_Yh=gpY​h=g. Any other such map has these same two coordinates at every zzz, so equals hhh. This also handles empty sets whenever the given maps exist.

    Suppose QQQ with projections qX,qYq_X,q_YqX​,qY​ also has this property. There is a unique u:P→Qu:P\to Qu:P→Q commuting with projections, and a unique v:Q→Pv:Q\to Pv:Q→P commuting with projections. The composite vuvuvu and idP\mathrm{id}_PidP​ have identical composites with pX,pYp_X,p_YpX​,pY​, so uniqueness for PPP gives vu=idPvu=\mathrm{id}_Pvu=idP​. Likewise uv=idQuv=\mathrm{id}_Quv=idQ​. Thus uuu is an isomorphism, and uniqueness in the universal property makes it the only isomorphism commuting with these projections. The qualification 'as cones' matters: the underlying sets can have other bijections.

    If this was the sticking point:

    • Proofs & Mathematical Writing — Practice existence-and-uniqueness proofs and equality of functions.
    • Set Theory — Review Cartesian products and composition of maps.

Already comfortable with this? Skip the first sections if you can write naturality squares without guessing arrow directions. Skip this route if you can prove uniqueness of universal objects and construct the free-group adjunction; reserve monads and Kan extensions for a specific later need.
