Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Start with ideals, localization, modules and Noetherian arguments. Compute examples before using geometric interpretations.

Branches to choose from

  • Local algebra and dimension
  • integrality
  • depth / Cohen–Macaulay structure
  • syzygies
  • computational ideals

How to continue

Move toward depth and resolutions for homological geometry, invariant rings for finite-generation questions, or computational methods for explicit equations. The first course remains useful across all branches.
