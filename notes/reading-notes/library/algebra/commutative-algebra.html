Commutative Algebra

交换代数：在一个点附近允许哪些分母？

独立中文导读 · 非逐句全文翻译 · 修订 2026-10-18

把整数里所有不被 333 整除的数变成可逆元，会得到什么？这里 222 可逆而 333 不可逆。反过来只允许 333 的幂作分母，得到的又是另一个环。先分清这两种具体局部化，比只记“局部化就是加分母”更能说明它的用途。

从一张分数表开始

需要环、理想、商环和一些模的语言；张量积可以在后续使用时补。主读物选 Milne 的 A Primer of Commutative Algebra：第 2 节理想按需复习，第 3 节诺特环、第 5 节分式环、第 6 节整性构成一条起步路线。不要把二十多个主题都当作开始代数几何前必须完成的清单。

先比较 Z(3)\mathbb Z_{(3)}Z(3)​ 与 Z[1/3]\mathbb Z[1/3]Z[1/3]：1/21/21/2、1/31/31/3 分别在哪里？再计算前者模去极大理想后得到的剩余域。接着解释诺特条件究竟约束的是理想的有限生成或升链，而不是“环里元素不多”。多项式环拥有无穷多个元素，并不妨碍诺特性。

带着几何或算术的问题继续

可以做两项检查：写出局部化的泛性质，并用它判断一个环同态能否延拓；证明某个具体元素在一个扩张中整于原环，明确给出首一多项式。若第一题只能列分数却说不清等价关系，先回局部化；若第二题把任意代数关系都当作首一关系，需要补“整”与“代数”的差别。

讲义有习题及部分编号题的解答附录，不代表全部题目都有完整解法。先按所读的第 2、3、5、6 节选题，再只查附录中确实列出的编号。需要几何动机时，可以同时去代数几何页面看 D(f)D(f)D(f) 与 AfA_fAf​，再回第 14 节的环谱，而不是先把谱的所有性质孤立记住。

若基础稳固，可按目标略过初步理想运算。数论路线接整数环与素理想分解，几何路线接局部环、层和维数；平坦性、同调方法等工具要在知道它们解决什么失败之后再加入，阅读会更有方向。

Studying Commutative Algebra

Start here · Choose a text · Practice · Check your understanding · Continue · Full reading list

Localization lets algebra inspect what survives near a chosen prime, while integral dependence controls how rings grow. Those two operations connect the early subject to geometry and number theory. Milne offers a short selection centered on ideals, Noetherian rings, fractions and integrality. Altman and Kleiman offer a more systematic term-sized development in ring and module language, which suits readers ready to keep tensor products and exact sequences active.

What to work on

  • Test prime and maximal ideals using quotients.
  • Construct a localization and track extension and contraction of ideals.
  • Use the ascending-chain condition and Hilbert's basis theorem.
  • Prove that a specified element is integral by finding a monic equation.

A starting text

A Primer of Commutative Algebra — James S. Milne. Read section 2, Ideals, as needed; then section 3, Noetherian rings, printed pages 9-13; section 5, Rings of fractions, pages 18-24; and section 6, Integral dependence, pages 25-29. Use section 14, The spectrum of a ring, only for the geometric continuation.

Choosing what to read

Calculate localizations of the integers and of a polynomial ring before stating their universal properties. Track an ideal through extension and contraction, noting the multiplicative set each time. Prove integrality with an actual monic equation, and keep a nonintegral example nearby. When using a chain condition, name the chain and show where it stabilizes. This makes the eventual spectrum of a ring a record of algebraic behavior rather than an unexplained topological object.

Try these before moving on

Try a proof or calculation before opening the hint. These solutions address the checks below, not the exercises in the linked textbooks.

  1. Describe Z localized at the complement of (5), determine its units, and list its prime ideals with justification.

    A hint

    Denominators cannot be divisible by 5. Use prime ideals of the integers disjoint from the denominator set.

    Work through the solution

    Let S=Z∖(5)S=\mathbb Z\setminus(5)S=Z∖(5). As a subring of Q\mathbb QQ, R=S−1Z={a/b:a,b∈Z, 5∤b}R=S^{-1}\mathbb Z=\{a/b:a,b\in\mathbb Z,\ 5\nmid b\}R=S−1Z={a/b:a,b∈Z, 5∤b}. If 5∤a5\nmid a5∤a, the inverse b/ab/ab/a lies in RRR. If 5∣a5\mid a5∣a, reduction modulo 555 sends a/ba/ba/b to 000 in F5\mathbb F_5F5​ and thus it cannot be a unit. Therefore the units are precisely the fractions whose numerators are also prime to 555.

    Prime ideals of a localization correspond by extension and contraction to prime ideals disjoint from SSS. The primes of Z\mathbb ZZ are (0)(0)(0) and (p)(p)(p) for positive primes ppp. The ideal (0)(0)(0) is disjoint from SSS, as is (5)(5)(5). For p≠5p\ne5p=5, the element ppp belongs to both (p)(p)(p) and SSS. Hence the only primes of RRR are (0)(0)(0) and 5R5R5R. The first is prime because RRR is a domain; the second is maximal since R/5R≅F5R/5R\cong\mathbb F_5R/5R≅F5​.
