Introduction to Representation Theory

Pavel Etingof et al. · Textbook · Introductory

Introduces representations using algebra and many exercises. Appropriate for strong undergraduates with linear algebra; the pace assumes comfort with abstract structures.

Preparation: Linear algebra; groups, rings and fields.

Lie Groups and Lie Algebras I

Pavel Etingof · Course notes · Intermediate

A graduate course connecting Lie groups, Lie algebras and representations. Initial manifold material helps orientation but does not remove the need for mathematical maturity.

Preparation: Abstract algebra; topology and smooth manifolds.

Open official PDF

Tensor Categories

Pavel Etingof, Shlomo Gelaki, Dmitri Nikshych and Victor Ostrik · Monograph · Advanced

Develops the categorical setting for representation theory. A specialist continuation rather than an introductory algebra text; link to the authorized author-hosted edition.

Preparation: Representation theory; category theory.
