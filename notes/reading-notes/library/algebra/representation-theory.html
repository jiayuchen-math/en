Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Start with group actions on vector spaces, intertwiners and finite-group characters before choosing a Lie or geometric branch.

Branches to choose from

  • Finite groups and characters
  • Lie algebras
  • Lie groups
  • invariant theory
  • tensor / geometric representation theory

How to continue

Roots and highest weights lead to Lie theory; locally compact groups require integration and analysis. Tensor categories and geometric representation theory also need categorical and homological tools.
