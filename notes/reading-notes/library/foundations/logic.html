Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Separate syntax from semantics: a formal derivation, a true statement in a structure, and a computable procedure are different objects.

Branches to choose from

  • Propositional / first-order logic
  • completeness
  • incompleteness
  • computability
  • higher-type computation

How to continue

After a first formal-logic course, branch into computability or into structures and definability. Research texts on higher-order computation need more than programming experience.
