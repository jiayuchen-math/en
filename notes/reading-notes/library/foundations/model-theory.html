Model Theory

Prerequisite path Preparation & skip guidance · Mathematical Logic & Computability · Set Theory · Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory

Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Begin with elementary equivalence, compactness, types and quantifier elimination in familiar algebraic examples.

Branches to choose from

  • Classical model constructions
  • stability
  • NIP
  • modules and definability
  • finite model theory

How to continue

Choose stability / NIP for structural questions, modules for algebraic connections, or finite model theory for finite structures. Compactness arguments do not automatically work over finite models.
