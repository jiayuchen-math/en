Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Start by translating quantified statements and testing examples. Practice writing short complete arguments before reading polished solutions.

Branches to choose from

  • Direct / contrapositive arguments
  • contradiction
  • induction
  • counterexamples
  • mathematical exposition

How to continue

Move from guided proof exercises to problems in algebra, analysis or combinatorics. A beautiful proof collection is enrichment, not an entrance requirement.
