Reading guide

Editorial orientation · optional routes Reviewed 2026-10-11

Separate elementary probability models from measure-theoretic constructions. Learn conditioning and convergence before advanced stochastic objects.

Branches to choose from

  • Probability foundations
  • Markov chains / mixing
  • networks
  • martingales / SDE
  • rough paths and stochastic PDE

How to continue

Choose stochastic analysis for continuous processes, high-dimensional methods for concentration, or networks / mixing for discrete processes. Singular stochastic PDE is not an entry-level probability topic.
