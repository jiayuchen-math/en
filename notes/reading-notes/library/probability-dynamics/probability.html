Introduction to Probability, 2nd edition

Charles M. Grinstead and J. Laurie Snell · Textbook · Introductory

Examples and exercises develop discrete and continuous probability, with Markov chains and generating functions. Reviewers note broad coverage; graduate measure theory lies beyond its starting point.

Preparation: Calculus; elementary counting.

Probability Theory: STAT310/MATH230

Amir Dembo · Course notes · Advanced

Graduate course notes for measure-theoretic probability. More concise than a first undergraduate textbook; readers should work through proofs rather than rely only on examples.

Preparation: Real analysis; measure theory.
