How to use this library

Choose a broad area, then a subject. Each resource includes its author, level, preparation and a short reading note. Follow the title to the full text or the official page providing it.

PDFs can be read in your browser and saved using the PDF viewer's download button. Some official sources offer HTML or require one further click to reach the PDF.

Levels are relative to the subject: an introductory geometry or algebra text may still require several undergraduate courses. Descriptions are editorial reading guidance based on author introductions, contents and linked reviews where available, not ratings or quotations.

Sources & access

Full-text links point to author, university, publisher or open-textbook sources, not unauthorized mirrors. Free access does not necessarily permit redistribution: most books are linked at their official source rather than copied here.

Existing print-only references are retained separately under “Further print reading”. Coverage spans major areas of pure mathematics but is not an exhaustive list of every research specialization.

Two older, permitted local archives remain available on the algebraic geometry and algebraic topology pages, with version and rights information. For current reading, use the official editions.
