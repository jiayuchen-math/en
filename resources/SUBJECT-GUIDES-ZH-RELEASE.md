# Chinese subject guides — 2026-10-18

29 subject guides now have independent Chinese editorial introductions. The three
short pilot introductions were expanded; the other 26 subjects were added. Each
uses a concrete mathematical question, an entry route, book/topic choices,
practice and self-check tasks, common pitfalls, selective review and continuation.
They are editorial advice, not complete courses or translations of linked books.

## Source and date scope

The sources of truth are `data/course-study-guides.json` and the existing reading
comparisons. Exact book and exercise URLs, locators and their recorded check dates
are reproduced from those records. The guide revision date is **not** a claim that
every chapter, exercise or answer was freshly audited. Existing practice limits,
including selected answers versus complete/instructor-only solutions, are retained.

During this work author/university/project pages or PDFs were opened for Hammack,
Open Logic (logic and set theory), Pillay, Axler (linear algebra and measure),
Judson, Milne (commutative algebra, course listings and algebraic number theory),
Baker, Riehl, Etingof, Vakil, UBC CLP, Lebl (analysis, complex analysis and ODE),
Melrose, Stanford EE261, Morris, Hatcher, Shifrin, Cannas da Silva, Stein's elementary
number theory, Kedlaya, Keller–Trotter, Grinstead–Snell and Teschl. These were
identity/scope/access checks, not a fresh line-by-line verification of all readings.

Milne's AG and MF PDF fetches were unavailable/time-out in the research tool;
the official course-note listing was used as corroboration. The existing URLs and
earlier chapter checks remain, rather than declaring the books unavailable or
assigning today's date to a PDF that was not inspected. Judson and Riehl's initial
landing-page requests failed; the direct table of contents / author PDF succeeded.

## Authorship and preservation

The Chinese prose was prepared with AI assistance and mathematical/source review;
it does not purport to be Jiayu Chen's personal experience or a professor's review.
Original prose follows the site's CC BY-SA 4.0 editorial licence. Linked works
retain their own terms and are not copied or translated in full.

No English course notes, original encyclopedia prose, original seven Chinese
study companions, bibliography facts, programme conditions or faculty records
were rewritten. Old URLs and stable IDs remain. The superseded three short
Chinese pilot texts remain in their original data file; the fuller Markdown guides
are the displayed subject introductions.

Chinese titles and topic descriptions are searchable using the same subject-page
IDs. English search summaries exclude hidden Chinese panels. Each guide links to
the actual primary reading, relevant exercises, its preparation page, and a
source-file edit / specific correction form. Empty reading links are not generated.
