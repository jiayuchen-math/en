Meaning in context

A smooth real bundle endomorphism of the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold whose square is minus the identity on every tangent space.

光滑流形切丛的光滑实丛自同态，在每个切空间上其平方等于负恒等映射。

Example

On R2\mathbb R^2R2, set J∂x=∂yJ\partial_x=\partial_yJ∂x​=∂y​ and J∂y=−∂xJ\partial_y=-\partial_xJ∂y​=−∂x​. Then J2=−id⁡J^2=-\operatorname{id}J2=−id, the usual complex structure.

Usage and distinctions

An almost complex structure is pointwise data and need not arise from holomorphic charts. Compatibility with a specified symplectic form is a separate positive-definiteness condition.
