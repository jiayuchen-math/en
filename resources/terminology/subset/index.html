Meaning in context

AAA is a subset of BBB if x∈Ax\in Ax∈A implies x∈Bx\in Bx∈B for every xxx.

若 A 的每个元素均属于 B，则 A 是 B 的子集。

Example

{2}⊆{2,5}\{2\}\subseteq\{2,5\}{2}⊆{2,5}.

Usage and distinctions

Use ⊆ inclusively; ⊊ requires inequality. The meaning of ⊂ varies between authors.
