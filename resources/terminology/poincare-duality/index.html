Poincaré 对偶

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a closed oriented n-manifold with chosen integral fundamental class, cap product with that class is an isomorphism from H^k(M;Z) to H_{n-k}(M;Z).

对选定整数基本类的闭定向 nnn 维流形，与该基本类取卡积给出 Hk(M;Z)H^k(M;\mathbb Z)Hk(M;Z) 到 Hn−k(M;Z)H_{n-k}(M;\mathbb Z)Hn−k​(M;Z) 的同构。

Example

On an oriented closed surface, degree-one cohomology is dual by cap product to degree-one homology.

Usage and distinctions

Noncompact manifolds require compact supports or locally finite homology; manifolds with boundary require the relative Lefschetz version.
