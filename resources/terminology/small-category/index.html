小范畴

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A category whose objects and all morphisms form sets in the chosen size convention.

在选定大小约定下，其对象和全部态射均构成集合的范畴。

Example

The category of a finite poset is small.

Usage and distinctions

Smallness is relative to a universe when universes are used; locally small is a weaker condition.
