Meaning in context

For a smooth manifold and a nonnegative integer k, a smooth section of the kth exterior power of its cotangent bundle; pointwise it is an alternating real multilinear function of k tangent vectors.

在光滑流形上，余切丛的第 kkk 次外幂的光滑截面，kkk 为非负整数；在每一点，它是关于 kkk 个切向量的交替实多线性函数。

Usage and distinctions

A differential form is alternating; a general covariant tensor need not be. The phrase exterior form does not assert that the form is an exterior derivative.
