拓扑传递

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: topologically transitive · 拓扑传递性

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A continuous self-map TTT is topologically transitive if for every nonempty open U,VU,VU,V some n≥0n\ge0n≥0 satisfies Tn(U)∩V≠∅T^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothingTn(U)∩V=∅.

连续自映射的任意两个非空开区域，存在某个非负迭代次数把前一区域的一部分送到后一区域。

Example

An irrational circle rotation and the full binary shift are topologically transitive.

Usage and distinctions

A dense forward orbit is equivalent in the usual compact metric setting without isolated points; do not use that equivalence without hypotheses.
