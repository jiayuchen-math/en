样本空间

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The set Ω\OmegaΩ of possible outcomes in a specified probability model.

给定概率模型中所有可能结果组成的集合。

Example

Two binary observations have sample space {0,1}2\{0,1\}^2{0,1}2.

Usage and distinctions

Outcomes are points; events are measurable sets of outcomes.
