Meaning in context

The cardinality of any basis of a vector space over its specified field.

在指定标量域上，向量空间任一基的元素个数。

Example

dim⁡RC=2\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C=2dimR​C=2, while dim⁡CC=1\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C=1dimC​C=1.

Usage and distinctions

Infinite dimension is a cardinal, not a largest finite independent-set size.
