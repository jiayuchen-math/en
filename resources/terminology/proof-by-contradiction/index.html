反证法

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: reductio ad absurdum

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

In classical logic, a proof of PPP obtained by deriving ⊥\bot⊥ from the premises together with ¬P\neg P¬P.

在经典逻辑中，暂设结论的否定并推出矛盾，从而确立结论的证明方法。

Example

A least positive rational rrr would be contradicted by the smaller positive rational r/3r/3r/3.

Usage and distinctions

For positive conclusions the method uses double-negation elimination; proving a negation from a contradiction is already intuitionistically valid.
