同伦

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: homotopy of maps

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A continuous map H from X times the unit interval to Y whose endpoint slices are the specified continuous maps X to Y.

连续映射 H:X×[0,1]→YH:X\times[0,1]\to YH:X×[0,1]→Y，其两端切片分别为给定的两个连续映射。

Example

For maps into a convex Euclidean set, H(x,t)=(1−t)f(x)+tg(x)H(x,t)=(1-t)f(x)+tg(x)H(x,t)=(1−t)f(x)+tg(x) is a homotopy.

Usage and distinctions

Continuity must be joint in x and t; based or relative homotopies impose additional fixed-point conditions.
