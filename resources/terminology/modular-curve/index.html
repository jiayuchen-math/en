Meaning in context

The modular curves are the Riemann surface Y(Γ)=Γ\HY(\Gamma)=\Gamma\backslash\mathcal HY(Γ)=Γ\H and its compactification X(Γ)X(\Gamma)X(Γ) by adjoining Γ\P1(Q)\Gamma\backslash\mathbb P^1(\mathbb Q)Γ\P1(Q); specify Y or X to distinguish them.

Usage and distinctions

The quotient has a Riemann surface structure even at elliptic orbits; it is not a smooth free quotient everywhere. This entry fixes the classical complex-analytic sense.
