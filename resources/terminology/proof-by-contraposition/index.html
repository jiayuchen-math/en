逆否法证明

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A classical proof of P→QP\to QP→Q by deriving ¬P\neg P¬P from ¬Q\neg Q¬Q.

通过证明逆否命题 ¬Q→¬P 来确立原命题 P→Q 的经典证明方法。

Example

To show n2n^2n2 odd implies nnn odd, show even n=2kn=2kn=2k has even square 4k24k^24k2.

Usage and distinctions

The converse Q→P is not the contrapositive; reversing an implication alone is invalid.
