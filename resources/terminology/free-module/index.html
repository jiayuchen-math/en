自由模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

An RRR-module with a basis, equivalently a direct sum of copies of RRR indexed by a set.

具有基、等价地同构于若干个环自身之直和的模。

Example

R3R^3R3 is free with its coordinate basis.

Usage and distinctions

Finitely generated modules need not be free; Z/2Z\mathbb Z/2\mathbb ZZ/2Z is not free over Z\mathbb ZZ.
