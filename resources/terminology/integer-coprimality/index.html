Other names / variants: relatively prime integers · coprime integers

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Integers a,ba,ba,b are coprime when gcd⁡(a,b)=1\gcd(a,b)=1gcd(a,b)=1.

两个整数的最大公因数为 111 时，称它们互素。

Example

888 and 151515 are coprime although neither is prime.

Usage and distinctions

For three integers, a joint gcd of one does not imply pairwise coprimality: use 6,10,156,10,156,10,15.
