可表函子

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A set-valued functor naturally isomorphic to Hom(-,A) in the contravariant case, or Hom(A,-) in the covariant case.

反变情形自然同构于 Hom(-,A)、或共变情形自然同构于 Hom(A,-) 的集合值函子。

Example

On sets, B↦BAB\mapsto B^AB↦BA is represented covariantly by AAA.

Usage and distinctions

A family of bijections without naturality is not a representation.
