Meaning in context

For a simple graph G=(V,E) and W ⊆ V, the induced subgraph G[W] has vertex set W and exactly the edges of E whose two endpoints belong to W.

对简单图 G=(V,E) 和 W⊆V，导出子图 G[W] 的顶点集是 W，边恰为 E 中两端都在 W 内的边。

Example

In a triangle, the induced subgraph on any two vertices is K2K_2K2​.

Usage and distinctions

This record uses the source’s term 导出子图. Deleting an edge while keeping both endpoints usually stops the result being induced.
