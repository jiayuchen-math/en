Meaning in context

For a bounded real function on [a,b], the Riemann integral is the common value of the supremum of its lower partition sums and the infimum of its upper partition sums, when those values agree.

闭区间上的有界实函数，若所有分割的下和之上确界与上和之下确界相等，此共同值称为黎曼积分。

Example

For f(x)=xf(x)=xf(x)=x on [0,1][0,1][0,1], n equal subintervals give lower and upper sums (n−1)/(2n)(n-1)/(2n)(n−1)/(2n) and (n+1)/(2n)(n+1)/(2n)(n+1)/(2n), hence integral 1/21/21/2.

Usage and distinctions
