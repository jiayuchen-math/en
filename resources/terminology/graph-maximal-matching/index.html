极大匹配

Other names / variants: inclusion-maximal matching

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A maximal matching is a matching not properly contained in another matching of the same graph.

极大匹配是不真包含于该图任何其他匹配的匹配，即不能直接添加一条边而仍保持匹配。

Example

In a−b−c−da-b-c-da−b−c−d, {bc}\{bc\}{bc} is maximal, but its one edge is fewer than the two edges in {ab,cd}\{ab,cd\}{ab,cd}.

Usage and distinctions

Maximality permits a better matching obtained by deleting and replacing edges; it does not certify maximum size.
