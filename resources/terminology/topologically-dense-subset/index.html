稠密

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A subset whose closure equals the entire ambient topological space.

闭包等于整个拓扑空间的子集。

Example

Q\mathbb QQ is dense in R\mathbb RR.

Usage and distinctions

Density does not imply having positive measure or containing an open interval.
