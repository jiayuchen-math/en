Other names / variants: measure-theoretic mixing · mixing

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A probability-preserving map is strongly mixing if μ(A∩T−nB)→μ(A)μ(B)\mu(A\cap T^{-n}B)\to\mu(A)\mu(B)μ(A∩T−nB)→μ(A)μ(B) for every fixed pair of measurable events A,BA,BA,B.

保概率变换在时间间隔趋于无穷时，任意固定两事件的交概率趋于各自概率之积的性质。

Example

The fair binary Bernoulli shift is strongly mixing by eventual independence of separated cylinder blocks.

Usage and distinctions

This fixed-event definition differs from uniform mixing coefficients of stochastic processes and from topological mixing.
