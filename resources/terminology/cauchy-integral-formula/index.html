柯西积分公式

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If f is holomorphic near the closed disk of radius r centered at a, then for every z in that disk's interior, f(z)=(2 pi i)^(-1) integral_{|w-a|=r} f(w)/(w-z) dw with counterclockwise orientation.

函数在闭圆盘附近全纯时，其内点值等于沿逆时针边界积分 f(w)/(w-z)，再除以 2πi。

Example

On ∣w∣=2|w|=2∣w∣=2, ∫w2/(w−1) dw=2πi\int w^2/(w-1)\,dw=2\pi i∫w2/(w−1)dw=2πi because f(1)=1f(1)=1f(1)=1.

Usage and distinctions

The evaluation point must be inside and the function holomorphic throughout the disk; enclosing other singularities invalidates this form.
