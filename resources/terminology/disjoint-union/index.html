Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For indexed sets, the set of pairs (i,x) with x in the i-th set, retaining the component index.

一族带指标集合的无交并是所有 (i,x)（x 属于第 i 个集合）的集合，保留元素的来源指标。

Example

{2}⊔{2}\{2\}\sqcup\{2\}{2}⊔{2} has two tagged members.

Usage and distinctions

Already disjoint subsets may use their ordinary union as a model; tagging handles overlapping inputs.
