Meaning in context

A matching in a finite simple graph is a subset of edges such that no two chosen edges share an endpoint.

有限简单图中的匹配是一个边子集，其中任意两条所选边都没有公共端点。

Example

In a−b−c−da-b-c-da−b−c−d, the edges ababab and cdcdcd form a matching of size two.

Usage and distinctions

Matching size counts edges. A matching of size k saturates exactly 2k vertices; it need not be maximum.
