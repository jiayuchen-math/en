勒贝格可测集

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A subset E of R^n is Lebesgue measurable if m*(A)=m*(A intersect E)+m*(A minus E) for every subset A of R^n, where m* is Lebesgue outer measure.

若任意测试集合 A 的外测度都等于它在 E 内外两部分的外测度之和，则 E 为勒贝格可测集。

Example

[0,1][0,1][0,1] and every subset of a Lebesgue null set are Lebesgue measurable.

Usage and distinctions

Lebesgue measurable does not mean Borel: completing Borel measure adds subsets of null sets.
