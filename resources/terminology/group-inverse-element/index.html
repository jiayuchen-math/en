Meaning in context

For ggg in a group with identity eee, the unique element g−1g^{-1}g−1 with gg−1=g−1g=egg^{-1}=g^{-1}g=egg−1=g−1g=e.

对群元素 ggg，与它在左右两侧运算均得到单位元的唯一元素。

Example

The additive inverse of 444 in Z\mathbb ZZ is −4-4−4.

Usage and distinctions

In a general monoid a one-sided inverse need not be a two-sided inverse.
