向量空间

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

An abelian group VVV with a unital scalar action of a field FFF, distributive in both inputs and satisfying (ab)v=a(bv)(ab)v=a(bv)(ab)v=a(bv).

配备域 FFF 的标量作用的交换群，该作用保持两种加法、满足乘法结合律且 1v=v1v=v1v=v。

Example

C\mathbb CC has real basis 1,i1,i1,i.

Usage and distinctions

Dimension changes when the scalar field changes; specify that field.
