奇异单形

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Community usage

Meaning in context

A continuous map from the standard n-simplex with ordered vertices into a topological space, for some nonnegative integer n.

从具有有序顶点的标准 nnn 维单形到拓扑空间的连续映射，n≥0n\geq0n≥0。

Example

A constant map Δ2→{p}⊆X\Delta^2\to\{p\}\subseteq XΔ2→{p}⊆X is a singular two-simplex.

Usage and distinctions

The map need not be injective; its image need not look like a geometric simplex.
