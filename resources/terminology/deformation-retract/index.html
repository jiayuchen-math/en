Other names / variants: strong deformation retract

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A subspace A of X for which a homotopy of the identity of X to a retraction onto A fixes every point of A at every time.

子空间 A⊆XA\subseteq XA⊆X，存在从 XXX 的恒等映射到映向 AAA 的收缩映射的同伦，且全程固定 AAA 中每一点。

Example

The punctured plane strongly deformation retracts onto S1S^1S1 by radial motion.

Usage and distinctions

Some authors use deformation retract without the fixed-subspace requirement; this record uses the strong convention.
