条件期望

Probability · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: 条件数学期望

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For integrable XXX and a sub-sigma-algebra G\mathcal GG, an integrable G\mathcal GG-measurable variable YYY satisfying ∫AY dP=∫AX dP\int_A Y\,dP=\int_A X\,dP∫A​YdP=∫A​XdP for every A∈GA\in\mathcal GA∈G; YYY is unique almost surely.

给定子σ代数时，与原变量在其中每个事件上积分相同的可积、该子σ代数可测的变量；它在几乎必然相等意义下唯一。

Example

For a fair die XXX, conditioning on parity gives E[X∣parity]=4E[X\mid\text{parity}]=4E[X∣parity]=4 on even outcomes and 333 on odd outcomes.

Usage and distinctions

It is a random variable relative to information; conditioning on one positive-probability event instead produces a number.
