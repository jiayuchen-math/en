Meaning in context

For a smooth n-manifold, the disjoint union of all its tangent spaces, with projection sending a tangent vector to its point and smooth local trivializations obtained by differentiating coordinate charts; it is a real vector bundle of rank n.

对光滑 nnn 维流形，将各点切空间作不交并，以切向量所在点为投影，并由坐标卡的微分给出光滑局部平凡化，所得秩为 nnn 的实向量丛。

Usage and distinctions

The rank is n, but the total-space dimension is 2n. Local product descriptions do not imply a global product description; the tangent bundle of S2S^2S2 is nontrivial.
