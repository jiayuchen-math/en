Meaning in context

A point xxx for which Trx=xT^rx=xTrx=x for some integer r≥1r\ge1r≥1; its least period is the smallest such positive integer, and the orbit is called periodic.

存在正整数次迭代后返回自身的点；其中最小次数是最小周期，经过该点的轨道为周期轨道。

Example

Under circle rotation by 1/31/31/3, every point has least period 333.

Usage and distinctions

A periodic point is one point; its periodic orbit (周期轨道) is the set of points visited through iteration. A least-period-r orbit has r distinct periodic points. A fixed point has period one; distinguish a return time from the least period.
