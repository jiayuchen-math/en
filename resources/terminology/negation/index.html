否定

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

In classical semantics, the connective reversing a statement’s truth value.

在经典语义中把命题的真值反转的联结方式。

Example

¬(3=4)\neg(3=4)¬(3=4) is true.

Usage and distinctions

Constructively, ¬P means a derivation of falsity from P, not an automatic decision of P.
