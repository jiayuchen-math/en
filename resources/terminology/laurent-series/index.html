洛朗展式

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A Laurent series centered at a is a two-sided power expansion sum_{k in Z} c_k(z-a)^k, with its negative- and nonnegative-power parts converging on a specified annulus.

洛朗展式是包含正负整数次幂的双边展开式，其正幂部分和负幂部分须在指定圆环内分别收敛。

Example

For 0<∣z∣<20<|z|<20<∣z∣<2, 1/[z(2−z)]=1/(2z)+∑n=0∞zn/2n+21/[z(2-z)]=1/(2z)+\sum_{n=0}^\infty z^n/2^{n+2}1/[z(2−z)]=1/(2z)+∑n=0∞​zn/2n+2.

Usage and distinctions

Negative powers are allowed; a Taylor expansion around a requires holomorphic extension through its center.
