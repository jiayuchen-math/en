乘法原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: product rule

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If a finite construction has k stages and every valid prefix has exactly n_i continuations at stage i, its number of completed outcomes is the product of the n_i, provided construction histories uniquely specify outcomes.

有限构造分为若干阶段，若第 i 阶段每个合法前缀都有恰好 n_i 种延续，且构造历史与结果一一对应，则结果数为各 n_i 的乘积。

Example

Three distinct books can be placed in order in 3⋅2⋅1=63\cdot2\cdot1=63⋅2⋅1=6 ways.

Usage and distinctions

The available objects may depend on earlier choices; the number of continuations must be uniform at each stage.
