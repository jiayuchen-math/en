Meaning in context

A nonzero nonunit rrr in a domain such that r=abr=abr=ab forces aaa or bbb to be a unit.

整环中不能分解为两个非单位乘积的非零非单位元素。

Example

222 is irreducible in Z[−5]\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}]Z[−5​].

Usage and distinctions

Irreducible need not mean prime in an arbitrary domain.
