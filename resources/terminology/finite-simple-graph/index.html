Meaning in context

A finite simple graph is a pair (V,E) with V finite and E a set of two-element subsets of V; edges are undirected, with no loops or repetitions.

有限简单图由有限顶点集 V 和二元子集构成的边集 E 组成；边无方向，不允许自环和重边。

Example

V={a,b,c}V=\{a,b,c\}V={a,b,c} and E={{a,b},{b,c}}E=\{\{a,b\},\{b,c\}\}E={{a,b},{b,c}} define a simple graph.

Usage and distinctions

Drawing crossings are not vertices unless included in V; parallel routes require a multigraph if their identities matter.
