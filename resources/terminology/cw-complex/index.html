CW-复形

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: cell complex

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A space built by attaching disks to successive skeleta along their boundary spheres and giving the union the weak topology determined by the skeleta.

逐层把闭球的边界粘到低维骨架上，并给最终并集赋予由骨架决定的弱拓扑所形成的空间。

Example

SnS^nSn has a CW structure with one zero-cell and one n-cell.

Usage and distinctions

A finite-dimensional CW complex can have infinitely many cells; finite-dimensional and finite are different conditions.
