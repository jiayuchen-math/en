控制收敛定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If measurable f_n converge almost everywhere to a measurable f and |f_n|<=g almost everywhere for one nonnegative integrable g, then f is integrable and the integrals of f_n converge to that of f.

可测函数列几乎处处收敛于可测函数 f，且绝对值由同一个非负可积函数控制时，f 可积并可交换极限与积分。

Example

On [0,1][0,1][0,1], fn(x)=xnf_n(x)=x^nfn​(x)=xn is bounded by 111 and tends to 000 almost everywhere; ∫fn=1/(n+1)→0\int f_n=1/(n+1)\to0∫fn​=1/(n+1)→0.

Usage and distinctions

Pointwise convergence alone is insufficient: n squared times the indicator of (0,1/n squared) tends to zero but has integral one.
