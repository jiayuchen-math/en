Meaning in context

For a surjection q from a topological space X onto a set Y, the topology in which U is open in Y exactly when its inverse image under q is open in X.

对满射 q:X→Yq:X\to Yq:X→Y，规定 U⊆YU\subseteq YU⊆Y 为开集当且仅当 q−1(U)q^{-1}(U)q−1(U) 在 XXX 中开，由此得到的拓扑。

Example

Identifying the endpoints of [0,1][0,1][0,1] yields a quotient homeomorphic to the circle.

Usage and distinctions

A continuous surjection need not give its target the quotient topology.
