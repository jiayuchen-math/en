Meaning in context

For F⊣GF\dashv GF⊣G, the natural transformation η:1C⇒GF\eta:1_C\Rightarrow GFη:1C​⇒GF whose component corresponds to 1FX1_{FX}1FX​ under the adjunction bijection.

对于 F⊣G，由伴随双射把 1_FX 对应到 η_X:X→GFX 而组成的自然变换 η:1_C⇒GF。

Example

For free vector spaces, ηS(s)=es\eta_S(s)=e_sηS​(s)=es​.

Usage and distinctions

Unit here belongs to an adjunction; it is not the multiplicative identity of a ring.
