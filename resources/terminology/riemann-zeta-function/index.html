Other names / variants: Riemann zeta

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The function ζ(s)=∑n≥1n−s\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}ζ(s)=∑n≥1​n−s for Re⁡s>1\operatorname{Re}s>1Res>1, continued meromorphically to C\mathbb CC with a simple pole at 111.

在 Re⁡s>1\operatorname{Re}s>1Res>1 由 ζ(s)=∑n≥1n−s\zeta(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-s}ζ(s)=∑n≥1​n−s 定义，并亚纯延拓到复平面的函数；在 111 处有一个单极点。

Example

ζ(2)=1+1/4+1/9+⋯=π2/6\zeta(2)=1+1/4+1/9+\cdots=\pi^2/6ζ(2)=1+1/4+1/9+⋯=π2/6.

Usage and distinctions

The defining series does not converge throughout the continuation domain; zeta at a negative argument means analytic continuation.
