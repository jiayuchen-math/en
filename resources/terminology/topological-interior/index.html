Other names / variants: interior

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The union of all open subsets of the ambient space contained in the given subset.

包含于给定子集的所有开集的并，亦即其最大的开子集。

Example

The interior of [0,1][0,1][0,1] in R\mathbb RR is (0,1)(0,1)(0,1).

Usage and distinctions

Interior is relative to the ambient topology; the same interval is all interior when it is the entire ambient space.
