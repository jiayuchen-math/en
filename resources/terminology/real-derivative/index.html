Meaning in context

For a real function defined near a, its derivative at a is the finite limit of (f(a+h)-f(a))/h as nonzero h tends to zero.

实函数在点 a 附近有定义，若增量商 (f(a+h)-f(a))/h 在 h 趋于零时有有限极限，该极限就是 a 处的导数。

Example

For f(x)=x3f(x)=x^3f(x)=x3, f′(2)=12f'(2)=12f′(2)=12.

Usage and distinctions

A two-sided derivative requires agreement of the left and right limits.
