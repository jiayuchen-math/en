Meaning in context

For events A,BA,BA,B with P(B)>0P(B)>0P(B)>0, the probability of AAA given BBB is P(A∣B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)P(A∣B)=P(A∩B)/P(B).

当条件事件B的概率为正时，A在B条件下的概率等于交事件的概率除以B的概率。

Example

For a fair die, P({6}∣{2,4,6})=1/3P(\{6\}\mid\{2,4,6\})=1/3P({6}∣{2,4,6})=1/3.

Usage and distinctions

The elementary ratio does not define conditioning on a probability-zero event.
