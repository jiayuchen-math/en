Hausdorff 空间

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: T2 space

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A topological space in which any two distinct points have disjoint open neighborhoods.

任意两个不同点都有互不相交的开邻域的拓扑空间。

Example

Any metric space is Hausdorff by taking balls of radius less than half the distance between two points.

Usage and distinctions

Closed singletons alone give T1 separation, which is weaker.
