Sylow 子群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a finite group of order pamp^ampam with prime ppp and p∤mp\nmid mp∤m, a subgroup of order pap^apa.

有限群的阶为 pamp^ampam 且 p∤mp\nmid mp∤m 时，阶为 pap^apa 的子群。

Example

A subgroup generated by a transposition is a Sylow 222-subgroup of S3S_3S3​.

Usage and distinctions

A maximal ppp-power divisor of the group order is required, not any ppp-subgroup.
