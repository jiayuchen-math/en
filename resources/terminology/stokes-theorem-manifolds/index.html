Stokes 定理

Other names / variants: generalized Stokes theorem

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For an oriented smooth n-manifold with boundary, n at least one, and a smooth compactly supported (n−1)-form ω, the integral of dω over the manifold equals the integral of the pullback of ω to its boundary, with outward-normal-first boundary orientation.

Example

On the unit disk oriented by dx∧dydx\wedge dydx∧dy, ω=(x dy−y dx)/2\omega=(x\,dy-y\,dx)/2ω=(xdy−ydx)/2 satisfies ∫Ddω=∫∂Dω=π\int_D d\omega=\int_{\partial D}\omega=\pi∫D​dω=∫∂D​ω=π for counterclockwise boundary orientation.

Usage and distinctions

The form must be smooth on the whole manifold, including its boundary. Compactness of the manifold makes the support condition automatic; noncompact manifolds need support or other justified conditions at infinity.
