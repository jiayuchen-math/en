Meaning in context

For square-integrable real X,YX,YX,Y, Cov⁡(X,Y)=E[(X−E[X])(Y−E[Y])]\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[(X-E[X])(Y-E[Y])]Cov(X,Y)=E[(X−E[X])(Y−E[Y])].

两个二阶矩有限变量的中心化乘积的期望。

Example

If Y=2XY=2XY=2X and Var⁡(X)=3\operatorname{Var}(X)=3Var(X)=3, then Cov⁡(X,Y)=6\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=6Cov(X,Y)=6.

Usage and distinctions

Zero covariance need not imply independence: symmetric XXX and X2X^2X2 can be dependent and uncorrelated.
