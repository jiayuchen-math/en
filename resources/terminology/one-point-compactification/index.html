一点紧化

Other names / variants: Alexandroff compactification

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a noncompact locally compact Hausdorff space X, the compact Hausdorff space formed by adding infinity, whose neighborhoods have compact closed complements in X.

对非紧局部紧 Hausdorff 空间添一个无穷远点；该点的开邻域的补集在原空间中紧且闭，由此得到紧 Hausdorff 空间。

Example

The one-point compactification of R\mathbb RR is homeomorphic to S1S^1S1.

Usage and distinctions

The closed-compact-complement construction exists more generally, but the stated hypotheses ensure the intended Hausdorff compactification and dense inclusion.
