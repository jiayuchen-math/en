Van Kampen 定理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: Seifert–van Kampen theorem

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a space covered by two path connected open sets with nonempty path connected intersection and a basepoint in that intersection, its fundamental group is the pushout of the two fundamental groups over that of the intersection.

空间由两个道路连通开集覆盖，交集非空且道路连通，基点位于交集中时，总空间基本群是两开集基本群沿交集基本群的推出。

Example

For two circles meeting at a point, suitable open neighborhoods give π1≅Z∗Z\pi_1\cong\mathbb Z*\mathbb Zπ1​≅Z∗Z.

Usage and distinctions

A disconnected intersection needs a groupoid version or additional bookkeeping; the stated group formulation cannot simply ignore it.
