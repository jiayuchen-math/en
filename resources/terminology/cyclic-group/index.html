Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A group generated by one element, so every element is an integer power of that generator.

可由单个元素生成、每个元素均为其整数次幂的群。

Example

Z/7Z\mathbb Z/7\mathbb ZZ/7Z is generated additively by 1ˉ\bar11ˉ.

Usage and distinctions

Cyclic groups may be infinite; cyclic does not mean finite.
