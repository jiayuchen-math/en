Meaning in context

A sequence 0→A→iB→pC→00\to A\xrightarrow{i}B\xrightarrow{p}C\to00→Ai​Bp​C→0 with iii injective, ppp surjective and im⁡i=ker⁡p\operatorname{im}i=\ker pimi=kerp.

首个非零箭头单射、末个非零箭头满射且中间处像等于核的模序列。

Example

0→Z→2Z→Z/2→00\to\mathbb Z\xrightarrow{2}\mathbb Z\to\mathbb Z/2\to00→Z2​Z→Z/2→0 is exact.

Usage and distinctions

Exactness does not imply splitting; an RRR-linear section is additional data.
