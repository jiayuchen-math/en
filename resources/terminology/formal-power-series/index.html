Meaning in context

Over a commutative ring R, a formal power series is a coefficient sequence written ∑ a_n x^n; equality is coefficientwise, addition is termwise, and multiplication uses finite convolution at each degree.

交换环 R 上的形式幂级数是写作 ∑a_n x^n 的系数列；相等与加法逐系数判定，乘法在每个次数采用有限卷积。

Example

∑n≥0n!xn\sum_{n\ge0}n!x^n∑n≥0​n!xn is a valid formal series over Q\mathbb{Q}Q.

Usage and distinctions

Formal existence does not assert convergence at any nonzero numerical argument.
