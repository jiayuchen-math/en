连续函数

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A map between metric spaces is continuous if, at every domain point, every positive output tolerance is met by some positive input tolerance.

度量空间之间的映射，若在定义域每一点，对任意正的输出误差都能选取正的输入误差使条件成立，则称为连续函数。

Example

f(x)=x2f(x)=x^2f(x)=x2 is continuous on R\mathbb RR.

Usage and distinctions

The tolerance may depend on the point; uniform continuity removes that dependence.
