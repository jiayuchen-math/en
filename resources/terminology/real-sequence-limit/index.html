Meaning in context

A real sequence has limit a if, for every positive epsilon, all terms beyond some index lie within epsilon of a.

实数列以 a 为极限，是指任给正误差，存在一个指标，使其后所有项与 a 的距离都小于该误差。

Example

an=2+1/na_n=2+1/nan​=2+1/n tends to 222.

Usage and distinctions

A finite limit differs from divergence to positive infinity.
