Meaning in context

The quotient of the multiplicative group of nonzero fractional OK\mathcal O_KOK​-ideals by the subgroup of principal fractional ideals.

非零分式 OK\mathcal O_KOK​-理想的乘法群对主分式理想子群的商群。

Example

Cl⁡(Q(i))\operatorname{Cl}(\mathbb Q(i))Cl(Q(i)) is trivial since Z[i]\mathbb Z[i]Z[i] is a principal ideal domain.

Usage and distinctions

It measures failure of ideals to be principal; it is different from the group of units of the integer ring.
