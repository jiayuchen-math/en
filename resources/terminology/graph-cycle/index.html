Other names / variants: simple cycle

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

In a simple graph, a cycle consists of distinct vertices v_1,…,v_k with k ≥ 3, consecutive pairs joined, and v_k joined to v_1; its length is k.

简单图中的圈由至少三个互不相同的顶点依次连接而成，最后一个顶点再与第一个相连；长度为顶点数，也为边数。

Example

The three edges ab,bc,caab,bc,caab,bc,ca form a cycle of length three.

Usage and distinctions

Traversing an edge and immediately returning does not form a cycle in a simple graph; reversing or rotating a traversal describes the same underlying cycle.
