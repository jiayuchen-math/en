Meaning in context

A sequence of real functions f_n on E converges pointwise to f if for each fixed x in E the scalar sequence f_n(x) converges to f(x).

实函数列在集合 E 上点点收敛，是指固定任一点 x 后，数列 f_n(x) 收敛于 f(x)；所需指标可以随 x 改变。

Example

xnx^nxn on [0,1)[0,1)[0,1) tends pointwise to zero, although its supremum is always 111.

Usage and distinctions

Pointwise convergence need not preserve continuity or imply uniform convergence.
