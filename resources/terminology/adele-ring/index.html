Meaning in context

For a number field KKK, the ring of tuples (xv)∈∏vKv(x_v)\in\prod_v K_v(xv​)∈∏v​Kv​ such that xv∈Ovx_v\in\mathcal O_vxv​∈Ov​ at all but finitely many finite places, with the restricted product topology.

Example

For K=QK=\mathbb QK=Q, (0,0,0,…)(0,0,0,\ldots)(0,0,0,…) and the diagonal tuple (1,1,1,…)(1,1,1,\ldots)(1,1,1,…) are adeles.

Usage and distinctions

The full product allows nonintegral components at infinitely many finite places; the adele ring imposes the almost-everywhere integrality condition.
