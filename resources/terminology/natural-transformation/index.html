Meaning in context

For F,G:C→DF,G:C\to DF,G:C→D, components ηX:F(X)→G(X)\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)ηX​:F(X)→G(X) satisfying G(f)ηX=ηYF(f)G(f)\eta_X=\eta_YF(f)G(f)ηX​=ηY​F(f) for every f:X→Yf:X\to Yf:X→Y.

对于 F、G:C→D，由分量 η_X:F(X)→G(X) 组成且对每个 f:X→Y 满足 G(f)η_X=η_YF(f) 的族。

Example

The projections A×A→AA\times A\to AA×A→A are natural in AAA.

Usage and distinctions

A collection of correctly typed components need not satisfy naturality.
