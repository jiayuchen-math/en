Meaning in context

A smooth section of the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold: it assigns to each point a tangent vector at that point, with smooth coefficient functions in every coordinate chart.

光滑流形切丛的光滑截面，即在每一点选取该点的切向量，且在每个局部坐标中各系数函数都光滑。

Example

On R2\mathbb R^2R2, X=−y∂x+x∂yX=-y\partial_x+x\partial_yX=−y∂x​+x∂y​ is smooth and tangent to each centered circle since X(x2+y2)=0X(x^2+y^2)=0X(x2+y2)=0.

Usage and distinctions

A vector field assigns vectors at all points, whereas a tangent vector belongs to one point. Smoothness is stronger than continuity of a section.
