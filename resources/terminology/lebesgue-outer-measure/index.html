Meaning in context

The Lebesgue outer measure of any E in R^n is the infimum of sums of rectangular volumes over all countable rectangle families covering E.

欧氏空间任意子集的勒贝格外测度，是所有覆盖该集合的可数矩形族的体积和之下确界。

Example

For a countable set {xk}\{x_k\}{xk​}, coverings with total volume below any ε>0\varepsilon>0ε>0 give outer measure zero.

Usage and distinctions

An outer measure is countably subadditive; countable additivity is guaranteed only after restricting to its measurable sets.
