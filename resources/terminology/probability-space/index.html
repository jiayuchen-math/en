概率空间

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A triple (Ω,F,P)(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)(Ω,F,P) in which F\mathcal FF is a sigma-algebra on Ω\OmegaΩ and PPP is a countably additive nonnegative measure with P(Ω)=1P(\Omega)=1P(Ω)=1.

由样本空间、事件的σ代数及总质量为1的概率测度组成的三元组。

Example

For a fair four-sided die, P({1,3})=1/2P(\{1,3\})=1/2P({1,3})=1/2.

Usage and distinctions

A sample space alone does not specify which events are measurable or their probabilities.
