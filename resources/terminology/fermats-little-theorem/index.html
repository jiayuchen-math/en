费尔马小定理

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: Fermat little theorem · 费马小定理

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If ppp is prime and p∤ap\nmid ap∤a, then ap−1≡1(modp)a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod pap−1≡1(modp); equivalently ap≡aa^p\equiv aap≡a for every integer aaa.

若 ppp 为素数且 p∤ap\nmid ap∤a，则 ap−1≡1(modp)a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod pap−1≡1(modp)；等价地，对任意整数 aaa 有 ap≡a(modp)a^p\equiv a\pmod pap≡a(modp)。

Example

36=729≡1(mod7)3^6=729\equiv1\pmod736=729≡1(mod7).

Usage and distinctions

The hypothesis that the modulus is prime matters; the little theorem differs from Fermat's last theorem about sums of powers.
