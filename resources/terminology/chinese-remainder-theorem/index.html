Meaning in context

For pairwise coprime positive integers m1,…,mrm_1,\ldots,m_rm1​,…,mr​, any prescribed residues have a simultaneous integer solution unique modulo ∏imi\prod_i m_i∏i​mi​.

对两两互素的正整数 m1,…,mrm_1,\ldots,m_rm1​,…,mr​，任意指定的剩余类都有同时满足的整数解，且解模 ∏imi\prod_i m_i∏i​mi​ 唯一。

Example

x≡2(mod3), x≡3(mod5)x\equiv2\pmod3,\ x\equiv3\pmod5x≡2(mod3), x≡3(mod5) gives x≡8(mod15)x\equiv8\pmod{15}x≡8(mod15).

Usage and distinctions

For noncoprime moduli, compatibility is required and the uniqueness modulus becomes the least common multiple.
