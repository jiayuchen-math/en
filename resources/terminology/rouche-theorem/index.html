Rouché定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If f and g are holomorphic near the closure of a bounded domain with piecewise smooth boundary and |g-f|<|f| on that boundary, then f and g have the same number of interior zeros, with multiplicity.

两个函数在有界区域闭包附近全纯，且边界上 |g-f|<|f| 时，它们在内部的零点个数相同，零点须按重数计数。

Example

On ∣z∣=2|z|=2∣z∣=2, f=z3f=z^3f=z3 and g=z3+z+1g=z^3+z+1g=z3+z+1 satisfy ∣g−f∣≤3<8|g-f|\le3<8∣g−f∣≤3<8; hence g has three zeros inside.

Usage and distinctions

The strict estimate is needed on the boundary, not throughout the interior; it counts zeros without locating them.
