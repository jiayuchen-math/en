等势

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: equipollence

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The relation between sets A and B that holds precisely when a bijection A→B exists.

集合 A 与 B 之间存在双射时成立的关系。

Example

N\mathbb NN and {0,5,10,…}\{0,5,10,\ldots\}{0,5,10,…} are equinumerous.

Usage and distinctions

Equal cardinality does not require sets to be equal or nested.
