Noether 环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A commutative ring in which every ascending chain of ideals stabilizes, equivalently every ideal is finitely generated.

每条理想升链最终稳定、等价地每个理想均有限生成的交换环。

Example

Z[x1,…,xn]\mathbb Z[x_1,\ldots,x_n]Z[x1​,…,xn​] is Noetherian for finite nnn.

Usage and distinctions

A Noetherian ring may have infinitely many ideals; stabilization is not finiteness of their collection.
