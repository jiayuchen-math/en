Other names / variants: weak convergence of probability laws

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Real-valued laws converge in distribution when their CDFs converge to the limiting CDF at all its continuity points.

实数概率分布的分布函数在极限分布函数的每个连续点上收敛。

Example

If ZZZ is a fair sign, (−1)nZ(-1)^n Z(−1)nZ has the same law for all nnn and thus converges in distribution to that law, while its paths alternate.

Usage and distinctions

The variables need not share a probability space; continuity points are essential when the limiting law has atoms.
