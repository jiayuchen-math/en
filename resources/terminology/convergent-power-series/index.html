Meaning in context

A power series centered at a is a series sum_{n>=0} c_n(z-a)^n with real or complex coefficients, studied as a function on the points where its partial sums converge.

分析中的幂级数由以同一中心为基点的非负整数次幂组成，在部分和收敛的点集上定义一个函数。

Example

∑n=0∞(x/3)n=1/(1−x/3)\sum_{n=0}^\infty (x/3)^n=1/(1-x/3)∑n=0∞​(x/3)n=1/(1−x/3) for ∣x∣<3|x|<3∣x∣<3.

Usage and distinctions

An analytic power series has a convergence domain; a formal power series in algebra is defined without numerical convergence.
