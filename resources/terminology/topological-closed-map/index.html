Meaning in context

A map between topological spaces that sends every closed subset of its domain to a closed subset of its codomain; it need not be continuous or surjective merely by this definition.

拓扑空间之间的映射，将定义域的每个闭集映为目标空间的闭集；该定义本身不推出连续性或满射性。

Example

The inclusion [0,1]↪R[0,1]\hookrightarrow\mathbb R[0,1]↪R is closed: its domain is compact and the target Hausdorff. It is not open since [0,1/2)[0,1/2)[0,1/2) is relatively open but not open in R\mathbb RR.

Usage and distinctions

A map with a closed graph need not be a closed map. Closedness is a property of direct images, whereas continuity tests inverse images.
