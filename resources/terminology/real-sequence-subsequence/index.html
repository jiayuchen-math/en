Meaning in context

A subsequence of (a_n) is (a_{n_k}) where the positive integer indices n_k increase strictly.

从实数列按严格递增的正整数指标抽取各项所组成的数列称为子列，原有次序必须保留。

Example

The even-index subsequence of (−1)n(-1)^n(−1)n is constantly 111.

Usage and distinctions

Reordering terms arbitrarily does not define a subsequence.
