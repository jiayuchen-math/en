线性映射的秩

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The dimension of the image of a linear map, measured over its scalar field.

线性映射的像作为标量域上的向量空间的维数。

Example

(x,y)↦(x,0)(x,y)\mapsto(x,0)(x,y)↦(x,0) has rank one.

Usage and distinctions

Rank concerns the image; nullity concerns the kernel.
