Meaning in context

The probability measure induced by a random variable XXX, given by μX(B)=P(X∈B)\mu_X(B)=P(X\in B)μX​(B)=P(X∈B) on Borel sets.

随机变量在取值空间上诱导的概率测度，即把每个Borel集合赋值为变量落入其中的概率。

Example

A fair binary indicator has mass 1/21/21/2 at each of 000 and 111.

Usage and distinctions

Identical distributions do not imply that two variables agree on their underlying probability space.
