Meaning in context

For a point p of a smooth real manifold M, the real dual vector space of its tangent space, consisting of real linear functions on tangent vectors at p.

Usage and distinctions

Covectors evaluate vectors; they are not canonically tangent vectors. A chosen Riemannian metric supplies such an identification, which changes if the metric changes.
