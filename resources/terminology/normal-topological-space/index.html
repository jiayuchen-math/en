T4 分离公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: normal separation

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A space in which any two disjoint closed subsets have disjoint open neighborhoods; T1 is not included here.

任意两个不交闭集均可被不交开集分别包含的空间；本条不附加 T1。

Example

Metric spaces separate disjoint closed sets using their distance functions.

Usage and distinctions

This is topological normality, unrelated to normal subgroups or Gaussian distributions; definitions including T1 are stronger.
