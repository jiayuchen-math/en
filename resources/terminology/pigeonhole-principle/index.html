鸽巢原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If n objects are assigned to m ≥ 1 categories, at least one category contains at least the ceiling of n/m objects; in particular n>m forces a repeated category.

将 n 个对象分入 m≥1 个类别，至少一类含有不少于 n/m 向上取整个对象；特别地，n>m 时必有一类至少含两个对象。

Example

Among nine integers, at least three have the same residue modulo four.

Usage and distinctions

The claim guarantees some category, not any designated category; distinct objects may have identical values.
