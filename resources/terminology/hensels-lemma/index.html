Meaning in context

For prime ppp and f∈Zp[X]f\in\mathbb Z_p[X]f∈Zp​[X], a simple root aaa of f mod pf\bmod pfmodp lifts to a unique root α∈Zp\alpha\in\mathbb Z_pα∈Zp​ with α≡a(modp)\alpha\equiv a\pmod pα≡a(modp).

对素数 ppp 及 f∈Zp[X]f\in\mathbb Z_p[X]f∈Zp​[X]，fff 模 ppp 的单根 aaa 可唯一提升为 Zp\mathbb Z_pZp​ 中满足 α≡a(modp)\alpha\equiv a\pmod pα≡a(modp) 的根。

Example

The simple root 333 of X2−2X^2-2X2−2 modulo 777 lifts to a 777-adic root congruent to 101010 modulo 494949.

Usage and distinctions

The derivative must be nonzero modulo p in this version; multiple roots require stronger hypotheses or different lifting statements.
