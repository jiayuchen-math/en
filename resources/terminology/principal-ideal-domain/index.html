主理想环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: PID

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A domain whose every ideal is generated by a single element.

每个理想都由单个元素生成的整环。

Example

F[x]F[x]F[x] is a PID when FFF is a field.

Usage and distinctions

A ring with principal ideals but zero divisors is not a principal ideal domain.
