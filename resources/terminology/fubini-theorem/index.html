Meaning in context

For sigma-finite measure spaces X and Y, an integrable real or complex function on their product has integrable sections almost everywhere, and its product integral equals either iterated integral.

两个 σ 有限测度空间上的乘积可积函数，其截面几乎处处可积，而且重积分等于任一顺序的累次积分。

Example

On [0,1]2[0,1]^2[0,1]2, ∫ ⁣∫xy dx dy=(1/2)(1/2)=1/4\int\!\int xy\,dx\,dy=(1/2)(1/2)=1/4∫∫xydxdy=(1/2)(1/2)=1/4.

Usage and distinctions

Fubini uses absolute integrability for signed functions; Tonelli permits a nonnegative integrand with an infinite integral.
