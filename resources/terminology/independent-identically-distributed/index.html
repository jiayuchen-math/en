独立同分布

Probability · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: i.i.d. · iid

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A family of random variables that is mutually independent and whose members all have the same probability law.

各成员具有相同分布且整个变量族相互独立。

Example

Independent repeated rolls of the same fair die give an i.i.d. sequence.

Usage and distinctions

Identical marginals do not imply independence: repeating one random variable at every time gives the same marginals but dependence.
