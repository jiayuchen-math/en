Meaning in context

For continuous f along a piecewise C^1 path gamma:[a,b] to C, its contour integral is integral_a^b f(gamma(t)) gamma'(t) dt, summed over the smooth pieces.

沿分段一阶光滑路径的连续复函数，其复积分由参数式 f(γ(t))γ'(t) 对参数积分定义，并对各光滑段求和。

Usage and distinctions

The factor gamma' includes orientation; this differs from arc-length integration against |gamma'|.
