Meaning in context

The constant-speed wave equation is partial_t^2 u=c^2 Delta_x u with c>0, classically for a twice differentiable space-time function; an initial value problem normally specifies both u and partial_t u at time zero.

常速波方程为 u_tt=c²Δu，其中 c>0；经典解要求相应二阶导数存在，初值问题通常同时给定初始位移和初始速度。

u(x,t)=cos⁡(3x)cos⁡(3ct)u(x,t)=\cos(3x)\cos(3ct)u(x,t)=cos(3x)cos(3ct) solves it since utt=−9c2u=c2uxxu_{tt}=-9c^2u=c^2u_{xx}utt​=−9c2u=c2uxx​.

Usage and distinctions

Prescribing displacement alone does not generally determine the solution; an independent initial velocity is needed.
