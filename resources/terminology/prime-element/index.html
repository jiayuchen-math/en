Meaning in context

A nonzero nonunit ppp in a domain such that p∣abp\mid abp∣ab implies p∣ap\mid ap∣a or p∣bp\mid bp∣b.

整环中整除乘积就必整除某一因子的非零非单位元素。

Example

xxx is prime in F[x]F[x]F[x].

Usage and distinctions

Prime implies irreducible; the reverse requires extra factorization hypotheses.
