Meaning in context

A smooth two-form on a smooth manifold whose exterior derivative is zero and whose contraction map from each tangent space to its dual is invertible.

光滑流形上的光滑二形式，外微分为零，且在每一点由缩并得到的切空间到其对偶空间的映射均可逆。

Example

The form dx∧dydx\wedge dydx∧dy on R2\mathbb R^2R2 is symplectic. The form x dx∧dyx\,dx\wedge dyxdx∧dy is closed but degenerates where x=0x=0x=0.

Usage and distinctions

An alternating form can be nondegenerate pointwise and still fail closedness in dimension at least four. A symplectic form is the additional structure; the pair with its manifold is a symplectic manifold.
