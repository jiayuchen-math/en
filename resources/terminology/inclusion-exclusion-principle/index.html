容斥原理

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: PIE · principle of inclusion and exclusion

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For finite sets A_1,…,A_m, the size of their union is the sum over nonempty index sets S of (−1)^(|S|+1) times the size of the intersection indexed by S.

对有限集合 A_1,…,A_m，并集大小等于各非空指标子集对应的交集大小按交集阶数交替加减所得的和。

Example

If ∣A∣=7|A|=7∣A∣=7, ∣B∣=6|B|=6∣B∣=6, and ∣A∩B∣=2|A\cap B|=2∣A∩B∣=2, then ∣A∪B∣=11|A\cup B|=11∣A∪B∣=11.

Usage and distinctions

In a complement formula the empty intersection is the specified universe; its sign is positive.
