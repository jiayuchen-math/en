Meaning in context

A Banach space is a real or complex normed vector space in which every Cauchy sequence for the norm distance converges to an element of the space.

Banach 空间是对范数所诱导的距离完备的实或复赋范线性空间，即每个基本列都有属于该空间的范数极限。

Usage and distinctions

Completeness depends on the norm; the same continuous-function space with its integral norm is incomplete.
