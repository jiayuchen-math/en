本原狄利克雷特征

Number Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A Dirichlet character modulo NNN that is not induced from a character modulo any proper divisor of NNN.

模 NNN 的狄利克雷特征，若不由模 NNN 的任何真因子的特征诱导而来，则称其本原。

Example

The nontrivial character modulo 333, with χ(1)=1\chi(1)=1χ(1)=1 and χ(2)=−1\chi(2)=-1χ(2)=−1, is primitive.

Usage and distinctions

Primitive means minimal modulus of induction; it does not mean a generator of the character group.
