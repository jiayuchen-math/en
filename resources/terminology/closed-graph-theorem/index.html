Meaning in context

An everywhere-defined linear map T between Banach spaces is bounded if its graph {(x,Tx)} is closed in their product norm topology.

在两个 Banach 空间之间处处有定义的线性映射，若其图像在乘积范数拓扑中为闭集，则该映射有界。

Example

The coordinate swap T(x,y)=(y,x)T(x,y)=(y,x)T(x,y)=(y,x) has a closed graph in R2×R2\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb R^2R2×R2 and norm 111.

Usage and distinctions

An operator defined only on a proper dense domain may be closed and unbounded; the theorem requires the full Banach domain.
