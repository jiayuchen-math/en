Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a subspace A of a topological space X, a continuous map from X to A whose restriction to A is the identity.

对拓扑空间 XXX 的子空间 AAA，连续映射 r:X→Ar:X\to Ar:X→A，其在 AAA 上的限制为恒等映射。

Example

The map r:S1→{1}r:S^1\to\{1\}r:S1→{1} that sends every point to 111 is a retraction. It cannot be the endpoint of a deformation retraction of S1S^1S1 onto {1}\{1\}{1} because their fundamental groups differ.

Usage and distinctions

A retraction supplies a continuous projection fixing the subspace; it supplies no homotopy from the identity. The Chinese name 收缩 here has this topological sense, rather than metric contraction.
