Meaning in context

Example

Suspending the identity on a single point with roof 111 gives unit-speed rotation of a circle.

Usage and distinctions

Compactness and a positive continuous roof give a positive minimum, preventing infinitely many roof crossings in finite time. Sarig's cited construction is a measurable suspension semiflow variant; this record fixes the topological compact-base flow convention. Noninvertible bases lead to semiflows. The inspected Chinese source calls suspension 扭扩, a documented naming variant.
