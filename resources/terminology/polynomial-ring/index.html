Meaning in context

For a commutative ring RRR, the ring R[x]R[x]R[x] of finite formal sums ∑aixi\sum a_ix^i∑ai​xi with coefficientwise addition and convolution multiplication.

以交换环为系数、由有限形式和构成并按多项式规则运算的环。

Example

x2+1∈F2[x]x^2+1\in\mathbb F_2[x]x2+1∈F2​[x].

Usage and distinctions

Over a finite field distinct formal polynomials may induce the same polynomial function.
