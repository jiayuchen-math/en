Meaning in context

An increasing family (Ft)(\mathcal F_t)(Ft​) of sub-sigma-algebras of a probability space's event sigma-algebra, indexed by ordered times: s≤ts\le ts≤t implies Fs⊆Ft\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_tFs​⊆Ft​.

概率空间内按时间递增的子σ代数族，用来记录截至各时刻可获得的信息。

Example

For coin observations ξj\xi_jξj​, Fn=σ(ξ1,…,ξn)\mathcal F_n=\sigma(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_n)Fn​=σ(ξ1​,…,ξn​) is their natural filtration.

Usage and distinctions

Completeness and right-continuity are additional conditions, not built into this definition; a dynamical flow is a different object.
