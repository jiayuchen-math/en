Meaning in context

A locally ringed space in which every point has an open neighborhood isomorphic, as a locally ringed space, to the prime spectrum of a commutative unital ring equipped with its structure sheaf.

每个点都有开邻域，连同限制的结构层同构于某个交换含幺环的素谱及其结构层的局部赋环空间。

Example

Spec⁡(k[ϵ]/(ϵ2))\operatorname{Spec}(k[\epsilon]/(\epsilon^2))Spec(k[ϵ]/(ϵ2)) for a field kkk is a one-point scheme with a nonzero nilpotent global section.

Usage and distinctions

The underlying prime-spectrum topology does not determine the structure sheaf; being affine, reduced, Noetherian or separated is not required in the general scheme definition.
