Meaning in context

The property that every sequence in a space has a subsequence converging to a point of that space.

空间中的任意序列都有收敛到该空间内某点的子序列。

Example

Every sequence in [0,1][0,1][0,1] has a convergent subsequence with limit in [0,1][0,1][0,1].

Usage and distinctions

Equivalent to open-cover compactness for metric spaces; not an unrestricted equivalence in topology.
