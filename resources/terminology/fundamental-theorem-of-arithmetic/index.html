算术基本定理

Number Theory · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: unique prime factorization of positive integers

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Every integer greater than one is a finite product of positive primes, unique up to the order of its factors.

每个大于 111 的整数都可分解为有限个正素数的乘积，且除因子次序外此分解唯一。

Example

84=22⋅3⋅784=2^2\cdot3\cdot784=22⋅3⋅7.

Usage and distinctions

Existence of irreducible factorizations alone is weaker than uniqueness; general domains need not satisfy this theorem.
