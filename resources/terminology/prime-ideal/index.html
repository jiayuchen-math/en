Meaning in context

A proper ideal p\mathfrak pp in a commutative ring with ab∈pab\in\mathfrak pab∈p implying a∈pa\in\mathfrak pa∈p or b∈pb\in\mathfrak pb∈p.

交换环中包含乘积就必包含某一因子的真理想。

Example

(0)(0)(0) is prime in Z\mathbb ZZ.

Usage and distinctions

Properness matters; the whole ring is never a prime ideal.
