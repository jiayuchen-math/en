T3 分离公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: regular separation

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A space in which a point and a disjoint closed subset can be enclosed in disjoint open sets; T1 is not included in this record.

点与不含该点的闭集可用互不相交的开集分开的空间；本条不附加 T1 条件。

Example

Every metric space satisfies this separation property.

Usage and distinctions

Some authors include T1 in regular or T3; the cited syllabus explicitly treats T1 plus T3 separately.
