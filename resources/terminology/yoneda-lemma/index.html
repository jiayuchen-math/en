Meaning in context

For small CCC, presheaf PPP, and A∈CA\in CA∈C, evaluation at 1A1_A1A​ gives Nat⁡(Hom⁡(−,A),P)≅P(A)\operatorname{Nat}(\operatorname{Hom}(-,A),P)\cong P(A)Nat(Hom(−,A),P)≅P(A) naturally.

对于小范畴 C、预层 P 和对象 A，在 1_A 处取值给出 Nat(Hom(-,A),P) 与 P(A) 的自然双射。

Example

For the poset 0<1<20<1<20<1<2 and constant presheaf {0,1}\{0,1\}{0,1}, exactly two transformations h1→Ph_1\to Ph1​→P exist.

Usage and distinctions

The direction of the hom-functor must match the variance of P.
