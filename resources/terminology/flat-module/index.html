平坦模

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A module over a commutative ring for which tensoring preserves every short exact sequence.

在交换环上，与之张量的函子保持所有短正合列的模。

Example

Q\mathbb QQ is flat over Z\mathbb ZZ.

Usage and distinctions

Flatness requires exact tensoring, not finite generation or existence of a basis.
