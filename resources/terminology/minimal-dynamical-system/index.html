极小系统

Dynamical Systems · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: minimality · 极小性

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A continuous self-map of a nonempty compact space is minimal when every forward orbit is dense in the whole space.

非空紧空间上的连续自映射，其每个点的正向轨道都在整个空间中稠密。

Example

Every irrational circle rotation is minimal.

Usage and distinctions

Transitivity needs open-set connections or, under suitable hypotheses, one dense orbit; minimality requires all forward orbits to be dense.
