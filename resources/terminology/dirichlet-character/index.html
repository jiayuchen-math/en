Other names / variants: Dirichlet multiplicative character

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For N≥1N\ge1N≥1, a homomorphism (Z/NZ)×→C×(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mathbb C^\times(Z/NZ)×→C×, extended periodically to integers and assigned 000 on integers not coprime to NNN.

对正整数 NNN，从 (Z/NZ)×(\mathbb Z/N\mathbb Z)^\times(Z/NZ)× 到 C×\mathbb C^\timesC× 的群同态；周期性延拓到整数，并在与 NNN 不互素的整数上取零。

Example

Modulo 444, χ(n)=0\chi(n)=0χ(n)=0 for even nnn, 111 for n≡1n\equiv1n≡1, and −1-1−1 for n≡3n\equiv3n≡3.

Usage and distinctions

Its restriction to units is a group character; the extension to all integers has zeros and is not a group homomorphism on their additive group.
