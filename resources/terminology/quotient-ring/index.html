商环

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For an ideal III of a commutative ring RRR, the cosets R/IR/IR/I with addition and multiplication induced by representatives.

对交换环理想 III，在陪集 R/IR/IR/I 上由代表元运算诱导得到的环。

Example

R[x]/(x2+1)≅C\mathbb R[x]/(x^2+1)\cong\mathbb CR[x]/(x2+1)≅C.

Usage and distinctions

For noncommutative rings a quotient ring requires a two-sided ideal.
