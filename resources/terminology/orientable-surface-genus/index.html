Meaning in context

For a connected closed orientable surface, the nonnegative integer g determined by its Euler characteristic through chi=2-2g; equivalently, the number of torus summands in its classification, with the sphere having genus zero.

对连通闭可定向曲面，由 Euler 数 χ=2−2g\chi=2-2gχ=2−2g 确定的非负整数 ggg；等价地，它是分类中环面连通和项的个数，球面的亏格为零。

Example

A connected sum of two tori has χ=−2\chi=-2χ=−2, so g=(2−(−2))/2=2g=(2-(-2))/2=2g=(2−(−2))/2=2. The sphere has χ=2\chi=2χ=2 and genus zero.

Usage and distinctions

The scope is closed orientable connected surfaces. Nonorientable genus counts crosscaps and obeys a different Euler formula; algebraic-curve genus is not defined by this unqualified surface formula.
