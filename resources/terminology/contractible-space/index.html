可缩空间

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A nonempty topological space whose identity map is homotopic to a constant map.

非空拓扑空间，其恒等映射同伦于一个常值映射。

Example

Every nonempty convex subset of Rn\mathbb R^nRn is contractible.

Usage and distinctions

Contractibility implies trivial positive homotopy and reduced homology, but trivial homology alone does not imply contractibility.
