乘积空间

Other names / variants: product space

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A Cartesian product equipped with the coarsest topology making every coordinate projection continuous; basic opens restrict only finitely many coordinates.

在笛卡尔积上赋予使各坐标投射连续的最粗拓扑；基本开集仅限制有限多个坐标。

Example

The product topology on R2\mathbb R^2R2 is generated by open rectangles.

Usage and distinctions

The Chinese headword names the equipped space; for infinite products, allowing unrestricted open conditions in every coordinate gives the generally finer box topology.
