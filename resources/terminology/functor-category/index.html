函子范畴

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For small C and locally small D, the category whose objects are functors C→D and whose morphisms are natural transformations.

当 C 小且 D 局部小时，以 C→D 的函子为对象、自然变换为态射、逐分量合成为态射合成的范畴。

Example

For a discrete two-object CCC, [C,Set][C,\mathbf{Set}][C,Set] is the category of pairs of sets.

Usage and distinctions

The smallness of C ensures transformation collections are sets in this formulation.
