Meaning in context

In ZFC, the assertion 2ℵ0=ℵ12^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_12ℵ0​=ℵ1​, where ℵ1\aleph_1ℵ1​ is the least uncountable cardinal.

在 ZFC 中断言 2^ℵ₀=ℵ₁，其中 ℵ₁ 是最小不可数基数。

Example

CH rules out a cardinal κ\kappaκ with ℵ0<κ<∣R∣\aleph_0<\kappa<|\mathbb R|ℵ0​<κ<∣R∣.

Usage and distinctions

Cantor’s uncountability theorem is provable in ZFC; CH is independent if ZFC is consistent.
