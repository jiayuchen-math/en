Meaning in context

In the zero-Dirichlet Poisson problem on bounded open Omega with f in L2, a weak solution is u in H_0^1(Omega) satisfying integral gradient u dot gradient phi=integral f phi for every phi in H_0^1(Omega); other PDEs require their own test identity and solution space.

Example

On (0,1)(0,1)(0,1), u=x(1−x)u=x(1-x)u=x(1−x) obeys ∫01u′φ′=2∫01φ\int_0^1u'\varphi'=2\int_0^1\varphi∫01​u′φ′=2∫01​φ, so it weakly solves −u′′=2-u''=2−u′′=2 with zero boundary values.

Usage and distinctions

A distributional identity without the specified H_0^1 membership does not impose this boundary condition or finite energy.
