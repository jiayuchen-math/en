Meaning in context

A measure space (X,A,mu) consists of a set X, a sigma-algebra A of its subsets, and a nonnegative extended-real function mu on A with mu(empty)=0 and countable additivity on disjoint measurable sets.

集合、其上的 σ 代数以及在该 σ 代数上非负、空集值为零且可列可加的测度，三者组成测度空间。

Example

([0,3],L,m)([0,3],\mathcal L,m)([0,3],L,m) with restricted Lebesgue measure has total mass 333.

Usage and distinctions

A measurable space has only a set and sigma-algebra; a probability space additionally has total measure one.
