Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A first-order formula obtained by applying a relation symbol to terms, or by equating two terms when equality is present.

由关系符号施用于适当个数的项，或在含等词时由两个项的等式形成的基本公式。

Example

R(f(c),x)R(f(c),x)R(f(c),x) is atomic for binary RRR.

Usage and distinctions

Atomic refers to syntactic construction; a complex term inside it does not make the formula non-atomic.
