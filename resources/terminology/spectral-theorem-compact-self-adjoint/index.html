Meaning in context

For a compact self-adjoint operator on a real or complex separable Hilbert space, an orthonormal basis of eigenvectors exists; nonzero eigenvalues are real with finite multiplicity and, if infinitely many, tend to zero when listed with multiplicity. Kernel vectors complete the basis.

实或复可分 Hilbert 空间上的紧自伴算子具有由特征向量组成的标准正交基；非零特征值为实数、重数有限，若按重数列出有无穷多个，则趋于零；算子核中的特征向量补全整个空间的基。

Example

For (Tx)n=xn/(n+1)(Tx)_n=x_n/(n+1)(Tx)n​=xn​/(n+1) on ℓ2\ell^2ℓ2 with n≥1n\ge1n≥1, the coordinate vectors form an eigenbasis and the nonzero eigenvalues 1/(n+1)1/(n+1)1/(n+1) tend to zero.

Usage and distinctions

This is the compact self-adjoint eigenbasis theorem, not the general spectral-measure theorem for arbitrary self-adjoint operators. Zero can be a spectral value without being an eigenvalue, and its eigenspace, when present, need not have finite dimension.
