Meaning in context

A simple function on a measurable space is a real measurable function taking finitely many values, equivalently a finite linear combination of indicators of measurable sets.

可测空间上只取有限多个实值的可测函数称为简单函数，也可写成有限个可测集指示函数的线性组合。

Example

21[0,1]−31(1,2]2\mathbf1_{[0,1]}-3\mathbf1_{(1,2]}21[0,1]​−31(1,2]​ is simple and has integral 2−3=−12-3=-12−3=−1.

Usage and distinctions

Simple functions may be constant on irregular measurable sets; step functions usually use interval or rectangle pieces.
