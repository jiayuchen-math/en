Meaning in context

An additive subgroup III of a commutative ring RRR with rI⊆IrI\subseteq IrI⊆I for every r∈Rr\in Rr∈R.

对环中任意元素的乘法均具有吸收性的加法子群。

Example

(2,x)(2,x)(2,x) is an ideal of Z[x]\mathbb Z[x]Z[x].

Usage and distinctions

An ideal need not contain 111; if it does, it equals the whole ring.
