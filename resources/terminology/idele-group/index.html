数域的伊代尔群

Other names / variants: idèle group

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a number field KKK, the restricted product of Kv×K_v^\timesKv×​ relative to Ov×\mathcal O_v^\timesOv×​ at finite places; algebraically it is AK×\mathbb A_K^\timesAK×​.

数域 KKK 的各 Kv×K_v^\timesKv×​ 关于有限素位处 Ov×\mathcal O_v^\timesOv×​ 的限制直积；在代数上等于阿代尔环的可逆元群。

Example

The diagonal tuple (2,2,2,…)(2,2,2,\ldots)(2,2,2,…) is an idele of Q\mathbb QQ, with inverse (1/2,1/2,1/2,…)(1/2,1/2,1/2,\ldots)(1/2,1/2,1/2,…).

Usage and distinctions

Nonzero components alone are insufficient: the tuple with component p at every finite prime p is an adele but not an idele.
