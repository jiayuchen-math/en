Meaning in context

For rate λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, a right-continuous counting process NtN_tNt​ starting at zero with stationary independent increments and Nt−Ns∼Pois⁡(λ(t−s))N_t-N_s\sim\operatorname{Pois}(\lambda(t-s))Nt​−Ns​∼Pois(λ(t−s)) for s<ts<ts<t.

从0出发、右连续的计数过程，不相交区间增量独立，s至t的计数增量服从参数λ(t−s)的泊松分布。

Example

For rate 333, the probability of no arrival in two time units is e−6e^{-6}e−6.

Usage and distinctions

A Poisson random variable describes one count; a Poisson process specifies the joint evolution of counts.
