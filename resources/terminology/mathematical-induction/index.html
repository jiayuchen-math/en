数学归纳法

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For PPP on N\mathbb NN, the principle that P(0)P(0)P(0) and every implication P(n)→P(n+1)P(n)\to P(n+1)P(n)→P(n+1) together yield ∀n P(n)\forall n\,P(n)∀nP(n).

自然数上的证明原理：零处成立且从任意 n 处成立能推出 n+1 处成立，则所有自然数处均成立。

Example

3∣(4n−1)3\mid(4^n-1)3∣(4n−1) follows from the base n=0n=0n=0 and 4n+1−1=4(4n−1)+34^{n+1}-1=4(4^n-1)+34n+1−1=4(4n−1)+3.

Usage and distinctions

A successor implication without a true base case proves nothing about all indices.
