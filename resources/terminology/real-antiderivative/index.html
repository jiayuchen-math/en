Meaning in context

An antiderivative of f on an open real interval I is a differentiable function F on I with F'(x)=f(x) at every point of I.

实函数 f 在开区间上的原函数，是在该区间可导并且导数处处等于 f 的函数 F。

Example

F(x)=x3/3+7F(x)=x^3/3+7F(x)=x3/3+7 is an antiderivative of x2x^2x2.

Usage and distinctions

On an interval, two antiderivatives differ by one constant; on a disconnected domain the constants may differ between components.
