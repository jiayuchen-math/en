Meaning in context

A complex number satisfying a monic polynomial with coefficients in Z\mathbb ZZ.

满足某个首一整系数多项式的复数。

Example

(1+5)/2(1+\sqrt5)/2(1+5​)/2 satisfies X2−X−1=0X^2-X-1=0X2−X−1=0.

Usage and distinctions

An algebraic number need not be integral: 1/21/21/2 satisfies 2X−1=02X-1=02X−1=0 but no monic integer polynomial.
