商映射

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A surjective map q of topological spaces such that a subset of the target is open exactly when its inverse image under q is open.

拓扑空间间的满射 qqq，满足目标中的子集开当且仅当其原像开。

Example

The endpoint-identification map [0,1]→S1[0,1]\to S^1[0,1]→S1 is quotient.

Usage and distinctions

A continuous surjection supplies only one direction of the defining equivalence.
