Meaning in context

A space in which every open neighborhood of a point contains a connected open neighborhood of that point.

任一点的任意开邻域内都含该点的连通开邻域的空间。

Example

Euclidean balls show Rn\mathbb R^nRn is locally connected.

Usage and distinctions

Being connected globally does not imply local connectedness, as the topologist's sine curve demonstrates.
