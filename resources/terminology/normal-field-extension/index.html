正规扩张

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

An algebraic extension L/KL/KL/K such that every irreducible polynomial over KKK having a root in LLL splits completely in LLL.

基域上的不可约多项式一旦在扩域中有根，就在其中完全分裂的代数扩张。

Example

Fp(t1/p)/Fp(t)\mathbb F_p(t^{1/p})/\mathbb F_p(t)Fp​(t1/p)/Fp​(t) is normal but inseparable.

Usage and distinctions

Normality does not imply separability or perfectness of either field.
