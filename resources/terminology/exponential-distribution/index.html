Meaning in context

For rate λ>0\lambda>0λ>0, the nonnegative law with survival probability P(X>t)=e−λtP(X>t)=e^{-\lambda t}P(X>t)=e−λt for t≥0t\ge0t≥0.

速率参数λ为正的非负连续分布，超过非负t的概率为exp(−λt)。

Example

With rate 222, P(X>1)=e−2P(X>1)=e^{-2}P(X>1)=e−2 and E[X]=1/2E[X]=1/2E[X]=1/2.

Usage and distinctions

Rate lambda is the reciprocal of the mean; some texts parameterize by the mean instead.
