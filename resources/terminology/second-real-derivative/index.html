Meaning in context

If f is differentiable near a and f' is differentiable at a, the second derivative f''(a) is the derivative of f' there.

若 f 在 a 附近可导且其导函数 f' 在 a 处可导，则 f' 在该点的导数称为 f 的二阶导数。

Example

For f(x)=x3f(x)=x^3f(x)=x3, f′′(2)=12f''(2)=12f′′(2)=12.

Usage and distinctions

Two derivatives need not imply a continuous second derivative.
