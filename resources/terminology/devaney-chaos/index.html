Devaney混沌

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a continuous self-map of an infinite compact metric space without isolated points, topological transitivity together with density of periodic points; these hypotheses imply sensitive dependence.

在无孤立点的无限紧致度量空间上，连续系统具有拓扑传递性且周期点稠密；在这些假设下敏感性可由二者推出。

Example

The full binary shift is Devaney chaotic by word concatenation and periodic repetition of words.

Usage and distinctions

Some definitions list sensitivity separately; always state the state-space assumptions, and do not equate this criterion with Li–Yorke chaos.
