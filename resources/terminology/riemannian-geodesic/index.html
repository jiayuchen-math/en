Meaning in context

A smooth parametrized curve on a Riemannian manifold whose velocity is parallel along the curve with respect to the Levi–Civita connection, so its covariant acceleration is zero; the parameter is affine.

黎曼流形上的光滑参数曲线，其速度关于 Levi-Civita 联络沿曲线平行，即协变加速度为零；参数采用仿射参数。

Example

On the unit sphere, γ(t)=(cos⁡t,sin⁡t,0)\gamma(t)=(\cos t,\sin t,0)γ(t)=(cost,sint,0) has ambient acceleration −γ(t)-\gamma(t)−γ(t), normal to the sphere, and hence zero tangential covariant acceleration.

Usage and distinctions

A geodesic need not minimize length between distant endpoints. A full great-circle traversal is a geodesic although its endpoints can coincide; arbitrary reparametrization may destroy the equation.
