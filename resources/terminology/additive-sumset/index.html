Meaning in context

For subsets A,B of an additively written abelian group, their sumset A+B is {a+b:a∈A,b∈B}; each resulting element is included once, regardless of representation multiplicity.

加法记号的阿贝尔群中，子集 A、B 的和集 A+B 是所有 a+b 构成的集合；同一个和值无论有多少种表示，都只计一个元素。

Example

{0,2}+{0,3}={0,2,3,5}\{0,2\}+\{0,3\}=\{0,2,3,5\}{0,2}+{0,3}={0,2,3,5}.

Usage and distinctions

A+A allows equal summands and is not the dilation {2a:a∈A}; restricted sumsets impose extra conditions not present here.
