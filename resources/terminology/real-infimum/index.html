Meaning in context

For a nonempty real set A bounded below, its infimum is the greatest real lower bound: it is no larger than any member of A and dominates every other lower bound.

非空且有下界的实数集，其下确界是所有下界中最大的实数；集合无有限下界时，可在扩展实数中记为负无穷。

Example

inf⁡{1+1/n:n≥1}=1\inf\{1+1/n:n\ge1\}=1inf{1+1/n:n≥1}=1, which is not a member.

Usage and distinctions

The infimum concerns all lower bounds, whereas a minimum is an attained least member.
