Meaning in context

A topological space whose topology is induced by some metric.

存在一个度量，其诱导拓扑恰为给定拓扑的空间。

Example

The usual circle is metrizable by the restricted Euclidean distance.

Usage and distinctions

A displayed metric is extra data; different metrics may induce the same topology while having different completeness properties.
