Meaning in context

A smooth map between smooth manifolds whose differential is injective at every point of its domain.

光滑流形之间的光滑映射，在定义域的每一点，其切映射均为单射。

Example

The map R→S1\mathbb R\to S^1R→S1, t↦(cos⁡t,sin⁡t)t\mapsto(\cos t,\sin t)t↦(cost,sint) is an immersion because (−sin⁡t,cos⁡t)(-\sin t,\cos t)(−sint,cost) never vanishes, but it is not injective.

Usage and distinctions

Immersion is a differential-rank condition. An embedding also requires a homeomorphism onto the image with its subspace topology; immersion alone imposes neither global injectivity nor that topology.
