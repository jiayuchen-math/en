范畴等价

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A functor F:C→D with a functor G:D→C and natural isomorphisms GF≅1_C and FG≅1_D.

具有拟逆函子 G 且 GF 与恒等函子、FG 与恒等函子分别自然同构的函子 F:C→D。

Example

Finite-dimensional kkk-vector spaces are equivalent to the category of natural-number dimensions and matrices.

Usage and distinctions

Equivalence allows composites naturally isomorphic to identities; isomorphism of categories requires actual inverse functors.
