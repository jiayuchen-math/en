基本群

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: pi-one

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The group of loops at a chosen point modulo homotopy fixing the endpoints, with multiplication given here by traversing the first loop then the second.

以指定点为基点的闭道路在定端同伦下的等价类组成的群；乘积约定先走第一条再走第二条。

Example

π1(S1,1)≅Z\pi_1(S^1,1)\cong\mathbb Zπ1​(S1,1)≅Z via winding number.

Usage and distinctions

Changing basepoint along different paths can change the comparison by an inner automorphism.
