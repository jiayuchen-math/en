狄利克雷特征的导子

Other names / variants: Dirichlet-character conductor

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a character modulo NNN, the least positive divisor fff of NNN from whose character group it is induced by reduction of units.

对模 NNN 的特征，其导子是使该特征可由模 fff 的特征经单位群约化映射诱导而来的最小正因子 f∣Nf\mid Nf∣N。

Example

The character modulo 888 with values 1,−1,1,−11,-1,1,-11,−1,1,−1 at 1,3,5,71,3,5,71,3,5,7 has conductor 444.

Usage and distinctions

The displayed modulus may exceed the conductor; extensions by zero at nonunits need not agree as integer-valued functions at primes removed from the modulus.
