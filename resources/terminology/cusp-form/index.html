Other names / variants: cuspidal modular form

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A holomorphic modular form on a congruence subgroup whose Fourier expansion at each cusp has zero constant term.

在同余子群的每个尖点处，傅里叶展开的常数项均为零的全纯模形式。

Example

Δ(τ)=q∏n≥1(1−qn)24\Delta(\tau)=q\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^n)^{24}Δ(τ)=q∏n≥1​(1−qn)24, q=e2πiτq=e^{2\pi i\tau}q=e2πiτ, is a weight-121212 cusp form on SL⁡2(Z)\operatorname{SL}_2(\mathbb Z)SL2​(Z).

Usage and distinctions

Vanishing at one cusp is insufficient if the subgroup has several inequivalent cusps; E_4 has constant term 1 and is not cuspidal.
