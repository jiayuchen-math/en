Meaning in context

A smooth manifold equipped with a smooth closed two-form whose value on every tangent space is nondegenerate; closed means its exterior derivative vanishes, and nondegenerate means contraction identifies tangent and cotangent spaces.

配有光滑闭二形式的光滑流形，且该形式在各切空间上非退化；闭指外微分为零，非退化指缩并给出切空间到余切空间的同构。

Example

On R2n\mathbb R^{2n}R2n, ω=∑i=1ndxi∧dyi\omega=\sum_{i=1}^n dx_i\wedge dy_iω=∑i=1n​dxi​∧dyi​ is closed and has invertible skew matrix, so it defines a symplectic manifold.

Usage and distinctions

Even dimension is necessary but does not itself provide a symplectic form. Unlike a Riemannian metric, the form is alternating and gives no lengths of individual vectors.
