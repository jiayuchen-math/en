Meaning in context

A compact topological manifold without boundary; manifolds here are Hausdorff and second countable.

紧致且没有边界的拓扑流形；此处的流形具有 Hausdorff 性和第二可数性。

Example

The sphere S2S^2S2 is closed. The compact disk D2D^2D2 is not a closed manifold because its boundary is S1S^1S1.

Usage and distinctions

Closed does not merely mean closed as a subset of some ambient space. The real line is closed in itself but is not a closed manifold because it is noncompact.
