Meaning in context

A graph H=(W,F) is a subgraph of G=(V,E) when W ⊆ V and F ⊆ E, with every edge of F having its endpoints in W.

图 H=(W,F) 是 G=(V,E) 的子图，意指 W⊆V、F⊆E，且 F 的每条边的端点都属于 W。

Example

A three-vertex path is an ordinary subgraph of a triangle after one edge is omitted.

Usage and distinctions

A subgraph may omit edges between retained vertices; an induced subgraph must retain all of them.
