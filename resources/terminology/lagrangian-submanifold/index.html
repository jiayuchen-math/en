拉格朗日子流形

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

An embedded smooth submanifold of a symplectic manifold of dimension 2n whose dimension is n and on whose tangent spaces the symplectic form vanishes. The submanifold need not be a closed subset under this entry's convention.

在 2n2n2n 维辛流形中的 nnn 维光滑嵌入子流形，其各切空间上辛形式均为零；本条不要求子流形在环境空间中为闭集。

Example

For ω=∑dqi∧dpi\omega=\sum dq_i\wedge dp_iω=∑dqi​∧dpi​ on T∗RnT^*\mathbb R^nT∗Rn, the graph pi=∂f/∂qip_i=\partial f/\partial q_ipi​=∂f/∂qi​ is Lagrangian: the symmetric Hessian cancels in the pulled-back two-form.

Usage and distinctions

Vanishing of the restricted form alone gives isotropy; the half-dimension condition is essential. Cannas da Silva uses a closed-embedding convention that is relaxed explicitly here.
