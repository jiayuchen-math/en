Meaning in context

A recurrence relation specifies each term of a sequence after a stated starting index in terms of earlier terms; an associated recursive specification also gives enough initial data to determine the sequence.

递推关系是在指定起始指标之后，用此前各项规定数列当前项的关系；递归定义还需给出足够的初始数据。

Usage and distinctions

The equation alone need not fix a sequence: a_n=2a_{n−1} has different solutions for different a_0. A recurrence need not be linear.
