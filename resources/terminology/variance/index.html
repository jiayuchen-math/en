Meaning in context

For E[X2]<∞E[X^2]<\inftyE[X2]<∞, the variance is Var⁡(X)=E[(X−E[X])2]\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-E[X])^2]Var(X)=E[(X−E[X])2].

二阶矩有限的随机变量与其均值之差的平方的期望。

Example

A fair 0/10/10/1 variable has variance 1/41/41/4.

Usage and distinctions

Variance has squared units; standard deviation is its nonnegative square root.
