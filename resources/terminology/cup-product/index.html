杯积

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: 上积

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The product on singular cohomology with coefficients in a commutative ring, induced by evaluating a p-cochain on the first p-face and a q-cochain on the last q-face of an ordered (p+q)-simplex and multiplying.

以交换环为系数时，在有序 (p+q)(p+q)(p+q) 单形的前 ppp 面与后 qqq 面分别计算余链并相乘，由此诱导的上同调乘积。

Example

On T2T^2T2, degree-one generators a,b can be chosen so a⌣ba\smile ba⌣b generates H2(T2;Z)H^2(T^2;\mathbb Z)H2(T2;Z).

Usage and distinctions

Graded commutativity holds on cohomology; the usual cochain formula is not literally graded commutative.
