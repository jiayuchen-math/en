Meaning in context

A real stochastic process for which every finite vector of observations has a multivariate normal distribution, allowing degenerate Gaussian vectors.

任意有限个时刻的观测组成的向量均服从多元正态分布的实值过程，允许退化情形。

Example

The constant-in-time process Xt=ZX_t=ZXt​=Z with Z∼N(0,1)Z\sim N(0,1)Z∼N(0,1) is Gaussian but not Brownian.

Usage and distinctions

Gaussian one-dimensional marginals do not by themselves guarantee that every observation vector is jointly Gaussian.
