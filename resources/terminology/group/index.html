群

Algebra & Algebraic Geometry · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A set with an associative binary operation, an identity, and a two-sided inverse for every element.

配备结合的二元运算、单位元且每个元素都有双侧逆元的集合。

Example

(Z,+)(\mathbb Z,+)(Z,+) has identity 000 and inverse −n-n−n for nnn.

Usage and distinctions

Commutativity is not a group axiom; it defines the additional abelian condition.
