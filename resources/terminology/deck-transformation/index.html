复迭变换

Other names / variants: covering transformation

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A homeomorphism of a covering's total space E satisfying p composed with that homeomorphism equals p.

复迭空间总空间 EEE 的同胚 hhh，满足 p∘h=pp\circ h=pp∘h=p。

Example

Integer translations are deck transformations of R→S1\mathbb R\to S^1R→S1.

Usage and distinctions

A deck transformation must preserve the projection, not just permute fibers by an arbitrary bijection.
