Parseval等式

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a 2 pi-periodic function f in L2 and coefficients c_k=(2 pi)^(-1) integral f(x)e^{-ikx} dx, Parseval's identity states (2 pi)^(-1) integral_{-pi}^pi |f|^2=sum_{k in Z}|c_k|^2.

对平方可积的 2π 周期函数，以通常平均积分定义 Fourier 系数时，Parseval 等式把周期内平方模的平均积分等同于所有系数平方模的和。

Example

For f=2+cos⁡xf=2+\cos xf=2+cosx, the coefficient sum is 4+1/4+1/4=9/24+1/4+1/4=9/24+1/4+1/4=9/2, matching the mean-square integral.

Usage and distinctions

Bessel's inequality gives only an upper bound for a general orthonormal system; equality requires completeness.
