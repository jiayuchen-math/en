泛性质

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A characterization of specified object-and-map data by a unique factorization for every competing compatible object-and-map datum.

用任意其他相容对象及映射资料的唯一分解，刻画指定对象及映射资料的性质。

Example

Maps X→A×BX\to A\times BX→A×B correspond uniquely to pairs of maps X→AX\to AX→A and X→BX\to BX→B.

Usage and distinctions

The structure maps belong to the data; uniqueness normally means up to a unique compatible isomorphism.
