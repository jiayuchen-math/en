Meaning in context

An isolated singularity of a holomorphic function is essential if it is neither removable nor a pole; equivalently its Laurent expansion has infinitely many nonzero negative-power coefficients.

全纯函数的孤立奇点若既不可去又不是极点，则为本性奇点；等价地，洛朗展开有无穷多个非零负幂项。

Example

e2/z=∑n=0∞2n/(n!zn)e^{2/z}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty 2^n/(n!z^n)e2/z=∑n=0∞​2n/(n!zn) has an essential singularity at 000.

Usage and distinctions

The classification requires an isolated singularity; a boundary accumulation of singularities is not classified by this trichotomy.
