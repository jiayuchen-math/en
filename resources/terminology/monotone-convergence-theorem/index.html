非负单调收敛定理

Analysis, Measure & PDE · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

If measurable functions 0<=f_n increase pointwise to f on a measure space, then their integrals increase to the integral of f, allowing the value infinity.

测度空间上的非负可测函数列逐点单调递增于 f 时，各函数积分的极限等于 f 的积分，允许为正无穷。

Example

On [0,1][0,1][0,1], fn(x)=min⁡(nx,1)f_n(x)=\min(nx,1)fn​(x)=min(nx,1) increases to 111 except at zero, and ∫fn=1−1/(2n)→1\int f_n=1-1/(2n)\to1∫fn​=1−1/(2n)→1.

Usage and distinctions

No single integrable dominating function is required; nonnegativity and monotonicity replace that hypothesis.
