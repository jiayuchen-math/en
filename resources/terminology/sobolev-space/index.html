Meaning in context

For open Omega in R^n, an integer k>=0, and 1<=p<=infinity, W^{k,p}(Omega) consists of Lp equivalence classes whose distributional derivatives of every order at most k are represented by Lp functions.

对欧氏空间开集、非负整数 k 及 1≤p≤∞，Sobolev 空间 W^{k,p} 由本身及直到 k 阶的分布导数均由 Lp 函数表示的几乎处处相等类组成。

Example

On (−2,2)(-2,2)(−2,2), u(x)=∣x∣u(x)=|x|u(x)=∣x∣ lies in W1,2W^{1,2}W1,2: its weak derivative is the sign function, with squared integral 444.

Usage and distinctions

Classical differentiability everywhere is unnecessary; a jump indicator has a Dirac derivative and is not in W^{1,p} across the jump.
