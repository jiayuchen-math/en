Meaning in context

A Z\mathbb ZZ-basis of the additive group OK\mathcal O_KOK​ for a number field KKK.

数域整数环 OK\mathcal O_KOK​ 作为自由阿贝尔群的一组 Z\mathbb ZZ-基。

Example

{1,(1+5)/2}\{1,(1+\sqrt5)/2\}{1,(1+5​)/2} is an integral basis of Q(5)\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)Q(5​).

Usage and distinctions

{1,5}\{1,\sqrt5\}{1,5​} is a Q\mathbb QQ-basis of that field but does not generate its full integer ring over Z\mathbb ZZ.
