Levi-Civita 联络

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: 黎曼联络

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a smooth Riemannian manifold, the unique connection on its tangent bundle that preserves the metric and has zero torsion: its covariant derivatives satisfy the metric product rule and their antisymmetric difference equals the vector-field Lie bracket.

光滑黎曼流形切丛上唯一的保度量、无挠联络；协变导数满足度量的乘积法则，其反对称部分等于向量场的李括号。

Usage and distinctions

Metric compatibility alone does not determine a connection. Christoffel symbols may be nonzero in curvilinear coordinates even for a flat metric.
