Meaning in context

For a nonempty real set A bounded above, its supremum is the least real upper bound: it bounds every member of A and is no larger than any other upper bound.

非空且有上界的实数集，其上确界是所有上界中最小的实数；集合无有限上界时，可在扩展实数中记为正无穷。

Example

A=(0,4)A=(0,4)A=(0,4) has sup⁡A=4\sup A=4supA=4, even though 4∉A4\notin A4∈/A.

Usage and distinctions

A maximum must belong to the set; a supremum need not be attained.
