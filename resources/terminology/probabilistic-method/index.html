Other names / variants: probabilistic existence method

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

The probabilistic method proves existence of a combinatorial object by defining a distribution on candidates and showing that the desired property has positive probability, sometimes after a controlled alteration.

概率方法通过在候选组合对象上定义分布，并证明所需性质有正概率出现来证明存在性；有时还需进行受控的修改。

Example

If a nonnegative integer-valued bad-configuration count X has EX<1\mathbb E X<1EX<1, some outcome has X=0X=0X=0.

Usage and distinctions

A sufficient expectation bound may fail even when a good object exists; an existence proof is not automatically an efficient construction algorithm.
