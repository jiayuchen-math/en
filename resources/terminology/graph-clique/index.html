Meaning in context

A clique in a simple graph is a vertex subset in which every pair of distinct vertices is adjacent, so its induced subgraph is complete.

简单图中的团是任意两个不同顶点都相邻的顶点子集，即其导出子图为完全图。

Example

Any three vertices of K4K_4K4​ form a clique of size three.

Usage and distinctions

A clique need not be inclusion-maximal or of maximum size. Counting vertices and counting its edges give different parameters.
