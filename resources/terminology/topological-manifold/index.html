拓扑流形

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a fixed nonnegative integer n, a Hausdorff, second countable space in which each point has a neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of real n-dimensional Euclidean space; boundary is excluded in this record.

对固定的非负整数 nnn，具有 Hausdorff 性和第二可数性的空间，每一点都有同胚于 nnn 维实欧氏空间开集的邻域；本条不含边界。

Example

The product S1×S1S^1\times S^1S1×S1 is a two-dimensional topological manifold: products of small open arcs give coordinate neighborhoods homeomorphic to open rectangles.

Usage and distinctions

No differentiability condition is imposed on chart transitions. Some authors omit second countability, yielding a broader convention; this entry includes it.
