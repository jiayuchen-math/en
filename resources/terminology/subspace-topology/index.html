子空间拓扑

Other names / variants: relative topology

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For a subset A of a topological space X, the topology on A consisting of intersections of A with open subsets of X.

对 A⊆XA\subseteq XA⊆X，由 AAA 与 XXX 的开集的所有交集构成的拓扑。

Example

[0,1/2)[0,1/2)[0,1/2) is relatively open in [0,1][0,1][0,1].

Usage and distinctions

Relatively open subsets need not be open in the ambient space.
