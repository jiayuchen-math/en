Meaning in context

For a linear map T:V→WT:V\to WT:V→W with VVV finite-dimensional, dim⁡V=dim⁡ker⁡T+dim⁡im⁡T\dim V=\dim\ker T+\dim\operatorname{im}TdimV=dimkerT+dimimT.

有限维定义域上的线性映射满足定义域维数等于核的维数与像的维数之和。

Example

Differentiation on F[x]≤2F[x]_{\le2}F[x]≤2​ in characteristic zero has nullity one and rank two.

Usage and distinctions

The finite-dimensional form uses ordinary integer addition; subtraction of infinite cardinals is not an equivalent formulation.
