李群

Geometry & Topology · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A group equipped with a finite-dimensional Hausdorff, second countable smooth manifold structure without boundary for which multiplication and inversion are smooth maps.

具有有限维、Hausdorff、第二可数的无边光滑流形结构，且乘法与求逆均为光滑映射的群。

Example

S1S^1S1 is a Lie group under complex multiplication; GL⁡(n,R)\operatorname{GL}(n,\mathbb R)GL(n,R) is one under matrix multiplication.

Usage and distinctions

Connectedness and compactness are additional properties. Its Lie algebra describes infinitesimal group structure but does not distinguish every pair of globally different Lie groups.
