C1 可数公理

Geometry & Topology · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: first countability

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A space in which every point has a countable basis of open neighborhoods: every open neighborhood contains a member of that basis.

每一点均有可数开邻域基，即该点的任意开邻域都包含基中的某个成员。

Example

Metric balls B(x,1/n)B(x,1/n)B(x,1/n) give such a basis at xxx.

Usage and distinctions

The basis may depend on the point; this is weaker than a countable basis for the whole topology. C1 is the source's notation.
