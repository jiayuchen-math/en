Meaning in context

A categorical structure with higher morphisms and coherent composition; the scoped (∞,1) model here is a simplicial set admitting fillers for every inner horn.

具有高阶态射和相容合成资料的范畴结构；本条限定的 (∞,1) 模型是每个内角都可填充的单纯集合。

Example

The nerve of an ordinary category and every Kan complex give (∞,1)(\infty,1)(∞,1)-category examples.

Usage and distinctions

Higher category is broader than (∞,1)-category; this entry fixes a model instead of claiming all higher theories share one definition.
