积

Category Theory · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Objects P with projections to a specified family such that every matching family of maps from any X arises uniquely from a map X→P.

配有到指定对象族的投影的对象 P，使从任意 X 出发的相应映射族都唯一来自 X→P 的态射。

Example

The product of sets {2}\{2\}{2} and {5,7}\{5,7\}{5,7} has two ordered pairs.

Usage and distinctions

The product includes its projections; in a poset it is a greatest lower bound.
