偏导数

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For f on an open subset of R^n, its jth partial derivative at a is the finite limit [f(a+h e_j)-f(a)]/h as nonzero h tends to zero, when that limit exists.

定义在欧氏空间开集上的函数，固定其他坐标，仅沿第 j 个坐标方向的增量商若有有限极限，该极限为相应偏导数。

Example

For f(x,y)=x2yf(x,y)=x^2yf(x,y)=x2y, ∂xf(2,3)=2⋅2⋅3=12\partial_xf(2,3)=2\cdot2\cdot3=12∂x​f(2,3)=2⋅2⋅3=12.

Usage and distinctions

Existence of every partial derivative at one point does not by itself imply total differentiability there.
