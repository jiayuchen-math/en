Meaning in context

Extremal graph theory studies optimal values of graph parameters under structural restrictions, such as the maximum edge count of an n-vertex graph avoiding a fixed subgraph.

极值图论研究图在给定结构限制下某个参数的最优值，例如固定顶点数并禁含指定子图时的最大边数。

Example

Among seven-vertex triangle-free graphs, the largest edge count is 12, attained by K3,4K_{3,4}K3,4​.

Usage and distinctions

Specify the optimized parameter and whether forbidden copies are ordinary or induced; those choices define different problems.
