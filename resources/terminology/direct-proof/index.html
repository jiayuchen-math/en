Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

An argument deriving the conclusion from the given hypotheses by justified steps, without adopting its negation as the main temporary assumption.

从已给假设出发，通过有根据的推理得到结论的一种证明方法。

Example

If a=5ra=5ra=5r and b=5sb=5sb=5s for integers r,sr,sr,s, then a+b=5(r+s)a+b=5(r+s)a+b=5(r+s).

Usage and distinctions

Proving an implication directly still permits assuming its antecedent; direct does not mean assumption-free.
