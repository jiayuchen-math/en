遍历变换

Dynamical Systems · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: ergodicity · 遍历性

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

A probability-preserving map TTT such that μ(T−1A△A)=0\mu(T^{-1}A\triangle A)=0μ(T−1A△A)=0 implies μ(A)=0\mu(A)=0μ(A)=0 or 111 for every measurable AAA.

保概率变换，其模零不变的任何可测事件只能具有概率0或1。

Example

An irrational circle rotation is ergodic for normalized length.

Usage and distinctions

Ergodicity depends on the chosen invariant measure and is weaker than weak mixing.
