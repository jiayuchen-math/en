Meaning in context

An inhomogeneous Littlewood–Paley decomposition splits a tempered distribution into smooth Fourier multiplier pieces using a low-frequency cutoff and dyadic annular cutoffs whose sum is one; the piece sum reconstructs the distribution in S′.

非齐次 Littlewood-Paley 分解，用一个低频截断和一列二进制尺度圆环上的光滑频率截断分割单位，将缓增分布拆成频率局部化片段，并在缓增分布意义下重构。

Usage and distinctions

The homogeneous decomposition omits a low-frequency block and requires attention to the zero frequency and polynomial ambiguity.
