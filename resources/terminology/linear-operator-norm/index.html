Meaning in context

For a bounded linear map T between normed spaces, its operator norm is sup{norm(Tx):norm(x)<=1}, equivalently the least constant in its global linear bound.

有界线性映射的算子范数，是单位闭球中所有向量之像范数的上确界，也就是线性界中最小的常数。

Example

For T(x,y)=(2x,−y)T(x,y)=(2x,-y)T(x,y)=(2x,−y) on Euclidean R2\mathbb R^2R2, ∥T∥=2\|T\|=2∥T∥=2, attained at (1,0)(1,0)(1,0).

Usage and distinctions

The matrix entry maximum is generally different; the operator norm depends on the norms chosen for domain and codomain.
