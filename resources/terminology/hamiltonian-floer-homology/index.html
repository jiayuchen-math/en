Meaning in context

In the closed symplectic setting where the symplectic form and first Chern class vanish on sphere classes, the homology over the field with two elements of the complex generated by nondegenerate contractible one-periodic Hamiltonian orbits, whose differential counts isolated finite-energy Floer cylinders modulo translation after regular choices.

在闭辛流形上，若辛形式和第一 Chern 类在球面类上均为零，取非退化的可缩一周期 Hamilton 轨道生成二元域上的链复形；经正则选择后，以模平移的孤立有限能量 Floer 圆柱计数定义边界，其同调即本条所指对象。

Example

For the standard symplectic two-torus and a sufficiently small suitable Morse Hamiltonian, the ungraded Floer homology has total F2\mathbb F_2F2​-dimension 1+2+1=41+2+1=41+2+1=4.

Usage and distinctions

This scoped construction concerns Hamiltonian periodic orbits. Lagrangian Floer homology uses different generators; manifolds with sphere bubbling require a broader construction and additional hypotheses.
