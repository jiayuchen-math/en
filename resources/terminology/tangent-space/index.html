Meaning in context

The tangent space at an interior point of a smooth manifold, or on a manifold without boundary, is the real vector space of velocities of smooth two-sided curves through that point, identifying curves with equal coordinate derivatives at time zero. At a boundary point it is the full vector space of real-linear derivations on smooth real-valued function germs, obeying the product rule at that point and using smooth extensions of half-space charts, rather than the cone of inward velocities.

在无边光滑流形上一点或带边流形的内点，切空间为经过该点的双侧光滑曲线速度所成的实向量空间，坐标中的零时刻导数相同的曲线等价。在边界点，采用光滑实值函数芽上满足该点乘积法则的实线性导子，借助半空间坐标的光滑延拓得到完整切向量空间，而非仅有朝内速度的锥。

Usage and distinctions

The tangent space belongs to one point and is an abstract vector space. A chosen embedding represents it in an ambient space; that representation is additional data.
