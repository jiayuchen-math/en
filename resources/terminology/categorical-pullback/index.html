拉回

Category Theory · Graduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: fiber product · 纤维积

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For A→C←B, an object P with maps to A and B making the square commute and universal among such commuting pairs.

对于 A→C←B，带有到 A、B 的态射、使相应方块交换，且对所有这种交换态射对具有唯一分解性质的对象。

Example

For inclusions A,B⊆CA,B\subseteq CA,B⊆C, their pullback in sets is A∩BA\cap BA∩B.

Usage and distinctions

A commuting square need not be a pullback; the universal property is additional.
