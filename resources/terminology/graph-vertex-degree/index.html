顶点度

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: degree of a vertex

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

In a finite simple graph, the degree d(v) of a vertex v is the number of its neighbors, equivalently the number of edges incident with v.

在有限简单图中，顶点 v 的度 d(v) 是与它相邻的顶点数，也等于关联于它的边数。

Example

The center of K1,4K_{1,4}K1,4​ has degree four; each other vertex has degree one.

Usage and distinctions

The neighbor-count equality uses simplicity. In multigraph degree conventions, parallel edges have multiplicity and a loop counts twice.
