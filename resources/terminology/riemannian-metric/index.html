Meaning in context

A smooth symmetric covariant two-tensor on a smooth real manifold whose value at every point is a positive definite bilinear form on that point's tangent space.

光滑实流形上的光滑对称二次协变张量，在每一点的切空间上给出正定双线性型。

Usage and distinctions

The tensor measures tangent vectors. The induced distance between points is obtained by infima of curve lengths; an arbitrary metric-space distance is not such a tensor.
