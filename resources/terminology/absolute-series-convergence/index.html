Meaning in context

A real or complex numerical series sum a_n is absolutely convergent when the nonnegative series sum |a_n| has a finite sum.

实数或复数项级数若各项绝对值组成的非负级数之和有限，则原级数绝对收敛。

Example

∑n=1∞(−1)n/3n\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^n/3^n∑n=1∞​(−1)n/3n converges absolutely because ∑3−n=1/2\sum 3^{-n}=1/2∑3−n=1/2.

Usage and distinctions

Conditional convergence means convergence of the original series without convergence of its absolute-value series.
