逻辑等价

Foundations & Logic · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

Two propositional formulas are logically equivalent when they have equal truth values under every valuation.

两个命题公式在所有真值赋值下均有相同真值时称逻辑等价。

Example

P→QP\to QP→Q is classically equivalent to ¬P∨Q\neg P\lor Q¬P∨Q.

Usage and distinctions

Agreement under one valuation is weaker than logical equivalence.
