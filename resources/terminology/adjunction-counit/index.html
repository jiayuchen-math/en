Meaning in context

For F⊣GF\dashv GF⊣G, the natural transformation ε:FG⇒1D\varepsilon:FG\Rightarrow1_Dε:FG⇒1D​ whose component corresponds to 1GY1_{GY}1GY​ under the inverse adjunction bijection.

对于 F⊣G，由伴随双射的逆把 1_GY 对应到 ε_Y:FGY→Y 而组成的自然变换 ε:FG⇒1_D。

Example

For free vector spaces, εV(∑aievi)=∑aivi\varepsilon_V(\sum a_i e_{v_i})=\sum a_i v_iεV​(∑ai​evi​​)=∑ai​vi​.

Usage and distinctions

The counit and unit have different domains; triangle identities do not make their components literal inverses.
