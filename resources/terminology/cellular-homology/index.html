Meaning in context

For a CW complex, the homology of the chain complex with C_n=H_n(X^n,X^{n-1};Z), whose boundary is formed from the relative connecting map followed by projection to the next skeletal pair.

对 CW 复形，取 Cn=Hn(Xn,Xn−1;Z)C_n=H_n(X^n,X^{n-1};\mathbb Z)Cn​=Hn​(Xn,Xn−1;Z)，以相对同调连接映射及向下一骨架对的映射组成边界，所得链复形的同调。

Example

A sphere with one zero-cell and one n-cell has cellular homology Z\mathbb ZZ in degrees 0 and n for n>0n>0n>0.

Usage and distinctions

This computes singular homology but uses a chosen cell structure; the differential depends on attaching maps, not just cell counts.
