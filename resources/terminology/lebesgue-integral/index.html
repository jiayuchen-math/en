勒贝格积分

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For nonnegative measurable f on a measure space, its Lebesgue integral is the supremum of integrals of nonnegative simple functions below f. For signed f, subtract the negative-part integral from the positive-part integral when they are not both infinite.

测度空间上非负可测函数的勒贝格积分，是其下方非负简单函数积分的上确界；一般实值函数按正负部分相减定义，但不允许两个无穷积分相减。

Usage and distinctions

Having a defined extended integral differs from being integrable, which requires a finite integral of the absolute value.
