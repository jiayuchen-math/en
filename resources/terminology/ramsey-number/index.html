拉姆齐数

Combinatorics · Undergraduate · Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted)

Other names / variants: Ramsey number · Ramsey数

Translation evidence: Literature usage

Meaning in context

For positive integers s,t, R(s,t) is the least positive N such that every red-blue edge coloring of K_N has a red K_s or a blue K_t.

对正整数 s、t，R(s,t) 是使 K_N 的每一种红蓝边着色都包含红色 K_s 或蓝色 K_t 的最小正整数 N。

Example

R(3,3)=6R(3,3)=6R(3,3)=6; coloring a five-cycle red and the other edges of K5K_5K5​ blue avoids both triangles.

Usage and distinctions

These are edge colors, not proper vertex colors. A one-vertex clique is monochromatic vacuously, giving R(1,t)=1.
