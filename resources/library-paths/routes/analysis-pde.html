Enter at the first unfamiliar stage. Follow a branch that matches your goal, and use the preparation checks only when you need a review.

1. Foundation: rigorous analysis

Calculation and proof are complementary; review only the skills that are missing.

  • Calculus
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can solve representative differentiation, integration and convergence exercises and explain the hypotheses used.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Proofs & Mathematical Writing
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Linear Algebra
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Real Analysis
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can work with epsilon arguments and prove standard compactness and convergence results, not just calculate limits.

    Open the prerequisite review

2. Bridge: integration and function spaces

Study convergence theorems and Banach/Hilbert spaces before using them in estimates.

  • Measure & Integration
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can apply monotone/dominated convergence and Tonelli/Fubini with their hypotheses checked.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Functional Analysis
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can use completeness, duality and basic operator examples; verify the specific text’s spectral or measure requirements.

    Open the prerequisite review

3. Core: differential equations

Start with examples, then study distributions, weak derivatives and Sobolev spaces in the subject reading guide.

  • Differential Equations & PDEs
    Preparation & skip guidance

    Skip real-analysis review if its listed skills are secure. Do not silently skip distributions, weak derivatives or Sobolev spaces needed by the chosen PDE text.

    Open the prerequisite review

4. Further branches

Choose elliptic, evolution or nonlinear problems; Fourier and harmonic methods provide tools for some, not all, branches.

  • Fourier & Harmonic Analysis
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You are comfortable with Lp estimates, Fourier transforms and interpolation; advanced multiplier topics remain separate.

    Open the prerequisite review
