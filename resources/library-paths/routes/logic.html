Enter at the first unfamiliar stage. Follow a branch that matches your goal, and use the preparation checks only when you need a review.

1. Foundation: mathematical language

Become fluent with quantifiers and proof methods.

  • Proofs & Mathematical Writing
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

    Open the prerequisite review

2. Core: logic and sets

Syntax, semantics and axioms give two complementary perspectives.

  • Mathematical Logic & Computability
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can distinguish validity from derivability and explain the relevant soundness and completeness statements.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Set Theory
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can distinguish countability, cardinality and order type; forcing-specific preparation is a separate step.

    Open the prerequisite review

3. Bridge: algebraic examples

This is useful preparation for the model-theoretic branch, not a universal prerequisite for all logic.

  • Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can construct quotient groups and rings correctly and state the assumptions of the isomorphism theorems.

    Open the prerequisite review
