Move from linear maps and group actions to representations and Lie-theoretic reading.

Enter at the first unfamiliar stage. Follow a branch that matches your goal, and use the preparation checks only when you need a review.

1. Foundation: linear structures

Become comfortable with invariant subspaces and changes of basis.

  • Proofs & Mathematical Writing
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can explain the quantifiers in a statement and write these forms of proof without relying on a worked solution.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Linear Algebra
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can prove rank–nullity, change bases and distinguish diagonalizable maps from general linear maps.

    Open the prerequisite review

2. Bridge: abstract algebra

Use groups, modules and quotient constructions.

  • Abstract Algebra & Galois Theory
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can construct quotient groups and rings correctly and state the assumptions of the isomorphism theorems.

    Open the prerequisite review

3. Core: representations and Lie theory

Begin with concrete finite-dimensional examples, then select the group or Lie-algebra branch.

  • Representation Theory & Lie Theory
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can construct representations, intertwining maps and basic decompositions; infinite-dimensional and Lie-theoretic preparation must be checked separately.

    Open the prerequisite review

4. Further tools

Add categorical or homological methods when your chosen representation-theory text uses them.

  • Category Theory
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can express a universal property and verify a natural transformation or an adjunction in a concrete example.

    Open the prerequisite review
  • Homological Algebra
    Preparation & skip guidance

    You can compute elementary homology and explain kernels, cokernels and exactness; deeper derived-functor gaps may remain.

    Open the prerequisite review
