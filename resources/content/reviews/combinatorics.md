## Additional English primary-source inspection

- [Keller and Trotter, §5.1](https://rellek.net/book-2016/s_graphs_intro.html): finite simple graph convention; neighborhood and degree; subgraph versus induced subgraph; path/cycle lengths; isomorphism; connectivity; tree/spanning tree; Theorem 5.9.

Printed pp.67–68 are PDF pp.83–84 for the latter section.
- [Green, Oxford C3.10 notes, 2024–25](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/C3.10-Oxford-2025.pdf): §0.1 Overview; §3.5 Definitions 3.10–3.11 (doubling and ordered, unnormalized energy), Proposition 3.12 and Cauchy–Schwarz proof; §4.1 Theorem 4.1 (integer Freiman theorem). The manuscript's displayed page numbering differs from PDF page numbering; the numbered sections/results are the stable locators used here.

## Final caveats and scope

The null graph is allowed as a simple graph, but the connected-graph/tree records explicitly require nonempty graphs. Empty perfect matchings and empty permutations use vacuous conventions. Matching size counts edges. Hall saturates a specified side; maximal and maximum are separate concepts. Ordinary and induced forbidden-subgraph conventions are distinguished. Formal generating functions do not assert convergence. EGF definitions state the factorial-division assumption. Additive energy counts ordered quadruples and sumsets include equal summands. The Freiman formulation is specifically in the integers and distinguishes parameter volume from cardinality.

The Chinese sample PDF extracts some inequality glyphs incorrectly (for example the Hall condition appears with a strict sign in extracted text). Its names are readable, and the mathematical Hall statement was checked separately against Keller–Trotter Theorem 14.7 and the NJU slide. No incorrect extracted inequality was copied into the content.

The author-notes URL for Huarui Zhou returned a forbidden page; BananaSpace and the original Whitman URL were inaccessible. The PKU-hosted random-walk PDF had garbled Chinese extraction and its screenshot failed. None is cited as definition or translation evidence. Some accessible student-blog material was inspected during discovery but is not used in the final evidence lists. No dictionary paragraphs or source exercises were copied.

The forty terminology IDs were compared with the other terminology files present at final editorial inventory: no same-ID collision involving combinatorics was present. This is a snapshot of a shared workspace, not a guarantee against later concurrent edits. All article prerequisites/related IDs resolve to records in this allocation.

Only eight assigned Markdown articles, terminology-combinatorics.json, and this review file were created or edited. No builder, automated test suite, generated HTML, or publication step was run. Main integration remains responsible for final source and rendering audits.
