# Algebra and number-theory primary-source review

Inspection date: 2026-10-14. Original editorial content is supplied under CC BY-SA 4.0. Contributor: Mathematics Resources editorial compilation (AI-assisted). This is a source and mathematical-content review, not independent peer review.

## Pilot delivered

Two encyclopedia articles: group; integer-greatest-common-divisor. Ten terminology records: five per area. The arithmetic factorization record requires a better inspected English definition citation and is not yet counted as source-complete.

## Sources actually inspected

- J. S. Milne, [Group Theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf): Chapter 1, Definition 1.1, Examples 1.3–1.4; Chapter 7 contents and character-table passage. Mathematical definitions use two-sided identities and inverses.
- Thomas W. Judson, [Section 2.2, The Division Algorithm](https://judsonbooks.org/aata-files/aata-html/integers-section-division-algorithm.html): gcd and relative-primality definitions, Theorem 2.2.2, Corollary 2.2.3, Subsection 2.2.1 and Example 2.2.4. Editorial examples use different numbers. The extension gcd(0,0)=0 is explicitly editorial.
- 李文威, [author source for Volume I, Chapter 4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wenweili/AlJabr-1/master/chapter4.tex): 半群, 幺半群与群, definitions labelled def:monoid and def:group; identity and inverse clauses and the paragraph naming 交换群 / Abel 群. These are original author materials, not a scraped third-party dictionary.
- 李文威, [author source for Volume I, Chapter 5](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wenweili/AlJabr-1/master/chapter5.tex): 唯一分解性, integer specialization, 整除关系, 最大公因子, 互素, def:UFD and 算术基本定理. Inspected text explicitly distinguishes irreducible and prime elements.
- 张神星, [初等数论习题课](https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/_resources/group1/M00/00/1A/rB_zR2jQEZ2Ac2vzAAOgvGtD4ZU844.pdf): title identifies the 2016 UCAS course; 第一章 4 补充, printed page 2, names 辗转相除法. Hosting institution is HFUT; it is not misattributed as an HFUT-authored course.
- 陈碧琴, [矩阵初等变换在初等数论中的应用](https://ngzk.cbpt.cnki.net/portal/journal/portal/client/paper/9d4357ca79270e825f7e92fa05f33846): inspected abstract and keywords contain 最大公因数; reference [5] title contains 最大公约数. This is Chinese-name evidence only; the article full text was not inspected.

## Access limitations and editorial conventions

The Volume I author PDF produced a redirect-loop/access error. Its author-maintained GitHub chapter sources opened successfully and are cited directly, with actual section headings and source labels instead of invented printed page numbers. Volume II PDF opened and identifies a CC BY 4.0 license. No translation is labelled Standardized: no normative standard has been inspected. Definition and translation citations occupy separate fields even when one author's bilingual mathematical source supports both.

No builder, tests, generated HTML, or publishing has been run. Only the assigned content and terminology destinations and this review note are edited.
