## English definition evidence inspected for the additions

- [Liviu I. Nicolaescu, Lectures on the Geometry of Manifolds](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~lnicolae/Lectures.pdf): author-hosted English text. Inspected §1.2.1, Definition 1.2.1 (smooth manifolds); §2.1.1, Definitions 2.1.2 and 2.1.4 (curve velocities and tangent spaces); §2.1.2 (tangent bundle); §2.1.3, Definition 2.1.20 (immersions/submersions); §2.1.4, Definitions 2.1.23 and 2.1.27 (vector bundles and sections/fields); §§2.3.1–2.3.2, dual/cotangent bundles, Definition 2.3.4 and Example 2.3.5 (forms and Riemannian metrics); §3.2.1, Proposition 3.2.3 (exterior derivative); §3.3.2, equation (3.3.7) (parallel transport); §3.4.2, Definition 3.4.4 and the following volume-form equivalence relation (orientation); §4.1.2, Proposition 4.1.9 and Definition 4.1.10 (Levi–Civita connection and geodesics); Definition 4.2.39 and Remark 4.2.40 (closed oriented surface genus); §7.1.3, Example 7.1.9 (de Rham complex). The actual §7.1.3 heading is **Very little homological algebra**, not Examples. Exact section and named-definition locators are preferred to unverified PDF offsets.
- [Ana Cannas da Silva, Lectures on Symplectic Geometry](https://people.math.ethz.ch/~acannas/Papers/lsg.pdf): Definitions 1.5–1.6 (§1.3), symplectic forms/manifolds; Definitions 3.2–3.3 and Proposition 3.4 (§§3.1–3.2), submanifold conventions and Lagrangian graphs; Definitions 12.4–12.5 (§12.3), almost complex structures and compatibility; Definition 18.1 (§18.1), Hamiltonian vector fields with contraction equal to positive dH. The Lagrangian row explicitly relaxes this source's closed-embedding convention. Immersion and topological embedding are kept distinct.
- [Will J. Merry, Floer theory](https://www2.math.ethz.ch/will-merry/files/Merry%20-%20Floer%20Theory%20(2014).pdf): Definition 2.24 and Theorem 2.25 (closed manifolds, sphere-vanishing assumptions, contractible periodic generators, coefficients modulo two, and the homology comparison); §4, Floer equation; §9, invariance. The row is scoped to this aspherical construction and does not assert an unrestricted definition for every symplectic manifold. The author's warning that these notes were not proofread is retained as a source-quality limitation, not presented as independent verification of Floer analysis.
- [Chris Wendl, Lectures on Holomorphic Curves in Symplectic and Contact Geometry](https://www.mathematik.hu-berlin.de/~wendl/pub/Jhol_arXiv.pdf): §2.1, Definition 2.8 and equation (2.2), printed p. 26. This defines pseudoholomorphic maps by complex-linearity of their differential. The row allows constant maps and branch points; it does not identify a parametrized curve with an embedded image.
- [Marco Gualtieri, MAT1300F Manifolds notes](https://www.math.toronto.edu/mgualt/courses/MAT1300F-2012/docs/1300-2012-notes-1.html): §1.1, Definition 1.1 and Example 1.4. Inspected the explicit Hausdorff/second-countable topological-manifold definition and torus example.
- [Michael Fochler, Math 330 - Additional Material](https://people.math.binghamton.edu/mfochler/bu-mfx/bu-math-arv/math-330-arv/math-330-2015-08/html/lec-notes/bu-ln-330-m330-solns-2015-10-04.pdf): title page identifies the author and last update October 4, 2015; §7.1.4, Example 7.6 (indiscrete topology) and Definition 7.12 (basis and neighborhood basis). The latter allows neighborhoods that are not open; the added row states this convention and contrasts it with the saved open-neighborhood row.
- [J. Peter May, Finite Topological Spaces](https://math.uchicago.edu/~may/FINITE/REUNotes2010/FiniteSpaces.pdf): §7, Definition 7.3, printed p. 11. Inspected local compactness defined by an open neighborhood contained in a compact subspace. No Hausdorff condition is inserted; the distinction records that other formulations may differ without separation.
- [Anton Petrunin, A minimalist intro to topology](https://anton-petrunin.github.io/topology/book.pdf), additional inspection: Chapter 2B (trivial and cofinite topologies); Exercise 2.12 in 2E (cocountable topology); Definition 5.9 in 5B (open/closed direct-image maps); Exercise 6.6 in 6A (open projection); Observation 9.15 in 9D (compact-to-Hausdorff closed maps).
- [Allen Hatcher, Algebraic Topology](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf), additional inspection: Chapter 0, retractions, printed p. 3; Example 0.4, real projective space, printed p. 6; §1.3, normal/regular covering definition and Proposition 1.39, printed pp. 70–71; §2.2, Example 2.36 and Euler Characteristic/Theorem 2.44; §3.3, manifold and closed-manifold definitions, printed p. 231, and Other Forms of Duality, manifold-with-boundary definition, printed pp. 252–253.
