# Number-theory encyclopedia source record

Actual inspection date: 2026-10-14. Original editorial compilation, AI-assisted; not external peer review. Main editor added eleven articles to the saved gcd article, giving twelve: eight Undergraduate, three Graduate, one Research. Terminology is handled separately; this record does not certify its unfinished snapshot.

## Scope and composition

The seven added undergraduate explanations are integer divisibility, the Euclidean algorithm, prime factorization, congruence, Fermat's little theorem, Euler's totient and the Chinese remainder theorem. Graduate bridges are number fields, Dirichlet characters and classical holomorphic modular forms. The research-oriented modular-curve entry explicitly scopes its analytic construction and separates it from arithmetic models and moduli functors. It is not a proof of modularity or a treatment of integral models.

Original worked examples include remainders for 119 and 51; an inverse of 35 modulo 12; prime exponents in 360; failure of cancellation modulo 8; exponent reduction modulo 13 and the base-two pseudoprime 341; totient values 18 and 20; a CRT solution modulo 28; conjugation in Q(sqrt(3)); a quartic character modulo 5; and modular-form parity under -I. These examples are editorial calculations rather than copied source exercises. Standard mathematical definitions and results are restated with their hypotheses, not reproduced as source prose.

## Primary passages inspected

- [Judson, Abstract Algebra: Theory and Applications, §2.2](https://judsonbooks.org/aata-files/aata-html/integers-section-division-algorithm.html): division theorem, divisibility and gcd paragraph, Theorem 2.2.2, Corollary 2.2.3 and Euclidean algorithm subsection. The gcd(0,0) convention is explicitly editorial.
- [Li, Methods in Algebra I, Chapter 5 author source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wenweili/AlJabr-1/master/chapter5.tex): 唯一分解性, `def:UFD`, following paragraph naming the integer specialization 算术基本定理, and prime-element paragraph. No printed page number is inferred from a TeX file.
- [Conrad, Fermat's Little Theorem](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/fermatlittletheorem.pdf): Theorem 1.2, elementary proof in Section 2 and testing limitations in Section 3.
- [Conrad, Euler's Theorem](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/eulerthm.pdf): Theorems 1.1–1.2, definition (1.1), unit-group interpretation, proof in Section 2 and prime-power counts in Section 3. General multiplicativity is justified using the independently inspected CRT, not attributed to an uninspected section.
- [Conrad, Chinese Remainder Theorem](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/blurbs/ugradnumthy/crt.pdf): Theorem 1.1 and Section 2 constructive proof and uniqueness.
- [Milne, Algebraic Number Theory, v3.08](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/ANT.pdf): opening number-field definition; Introduction's ring-of-integers discussion; Chapter 1 CRT passage and Theorem 1.14; Chapter 2 trace/norm passages, Proposition 2.19 and Corollary 2.20. Claims about full integer rings and ideals are distinguished from arbitrary orders.
- [Milne, Group Theory](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf): Theorem 1.26 and Corollary 1.27 for the finite-group proof of Fermat. The notes were not cited as a direct definition of the totient.
- [Zhang Shenxing, Algebraic Number Theory notes, v2.5.0.0, 2024-01-09](https://faculty.hfut.edu.cn/_resources/group1/M00/00/1A/rB_zSGjQAcqAWCrPACd7v7jEBRk596.pdf): Chapter 1 opening, Definition 1.1, Definition 1.14 and quadratic-integer example; §1.3.2 class/unit discussion; §4.1.1 Definitions 4.2–4.4, printed page 103. Character orthogonality in the article is derived directly; no unsourced analytic continuation proof is claimed.
- [Milne, Modular Functions and Modular Forms, v1.31](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/MF.pdf): analytic quotient and lattice/moduli discussion in the Introduction; Chapter 2; Chapter 4 Definition 4.5 and Proposition 4.7; Chapter 6 opening and arithmetic equations; Chapter 8 Definition 8.1 and Remark 8.3. The article's weight k convention differs explicitly from the source's 2k notation.
- [Li, Introduction to Modular Forms, Chapter 2 author source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wenweili/Modulform/master/chap2.tex): `sec:j-invariant`, `def:Delta` and following weight-twelve cusp-form proposition.
- [Li, same work, Chapter 3 author source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wenweili/Modulform/master/chap3.tex): `sec:Y-charts`, its ending paragraph naming open modular curves, `sec:X-charts` and `sec:cong-compactification`. Finite stabilizers and coarse versus fine moduli are not silently ignored.
- [Xinxiang University syllabus](https://sx.xxu.edu.cn/chudengshulun-110823.pdf): Chapters 1, 3 and 4 named curriculum headings. This is Chinese teaching usage only, not a definition or proof source, and not normative terminology evidence.
