# Subject depth, undergraduate routes and researcher expansion

One-time source review on October 11, 2026. This supplements the earlier review; it does not overwrite that audit or establish continuous monitoring.

## Delivered catalogue

- 400 distinct works, including 369 long-form works. The latest 161 additions are outside algebraic geometry.
- 29 subject pages with optional editorial branch-by-branch guides, level-grouped additions, preparation links and skip advice. Each has at least 12 listed or explicitly cross-referenced works; shared classifications do not increase the distinct count. Algebraic geometry remains at 33.
- 200 study/research routes: 44 undergraduate, 88 master, 55 doctorate, 4 preparatory, 7 postdoctoral and 2 visiting routes.
- Latest route additions: 29 US undergraduate, 6 undergraduate routes elsewhere, 10 mainland-China doctoral, 5 mainland-China postdoctoral and one BICMR preparation semester.
- 100 researchers; 43 explicitly published professional emails with a source and review date; 3 researchers have qualifying public completion records. No individual current student vacancy is confirmed.

## Scope and source handling

The existing 52 baseline resource records, old course-note text and PDF archives remain untouched. Publisher and author sources identify each new book. New prose and prerequisite guidance are editorial; no “widely taught” claim is inferred from reputation. Complete author or institutional PDFs are distinguished from source pages, paid access, previews and front matter. TIFR PDFs are authorized for individual use, not repository redistribution. Multi-volume sequences, reprints and cross-classification do not add duplicate counts.

Automated source access occasionally failed even when the institution or publisher indexed and advertised the exact resource. Examples include a challenged Penn catalogue, a refused Routledge page and intermittently unavailable academic PDF hosts. Such failures are not declared dead links based solely on a status code. The directory includes the official route and, where relevant, the uncertainty. No certificate, login, CAPTCHA or paywall bypass was used.

## Important limitations

- US university/college entry and mathematics-major declaration are separate. BA/BS tracks within the same first-year route are counted once. SAT/ACT rules are only asserted where the reviewed current checklist supports them; no score/GPA cutoff is invented.
- Most new undergraduate fees, aid awards, next-year deadlines and mathematics-specific cohorts are unverified. Unknown fields remain explicit. State residence, citizenship and a student visa do not automatically establish local tuition.
- Mainland domestic doctorate, recommended direct doctorate, international admission and Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan categories must be checked separately. Historical calls are not labelled current-open opportunities.
- PKU’s 86 and Fudan’s approximate 60/20 doctoral numbers are historical 2025/2026 allocations, not 2027 capacity. Shenzhen’s historical 2026 notice contains a conflicting email-material year. BICMR’s roughly 30 participants are a spring-2027 preparation cohort, not doctoral places.
- BICMR’s January-2026 postdoc notice states whole-year applications and CNY 240,000–320,000 annually before tax; actual offers and additional competitive fellowships must be confirmed. AMSS has an undated standing call with age, PhD-age and internal-mobility conditions. YMSC’s page retains 2025-start prose; linked calls govern. Fudan’s mathematical station page establishes the station, not a verified individual vacancy.
- Current institutional faculty listings are cross-checked against official profiles. Some biographies retain older ranks or concurrent affiliations. Exact tenure category, current supervision eligibility and all concurrent appointments are not universally established. YMSC’s generic centre inbox is never assigned to an individual.
- Amie Wilkinson explicitly prefers her professionally posted Gmail contact and warns that two old university addresses do not forward. Emmanuel Kowalski’s described 2026 postdoc has been filled. Ewain Gwynne’s listed June-2025 student completion is not a recruitment/start event.

## Validation

Structural checks cover distinct counts, semantic duplicates, retained original objects, every internal link/anchor, external new-tab attributes, access metadata, source fields, email provenance and 29 single-instance guides. Four directory controls are tested for search, compound filtering, reset, pagination and keyboard focus. Date uncertainty, eligibility/fee caveats and public-record boundaries are regression-tested. Complete entries are present in static HTML, not loaded only by JavaScript.

Publication uses the latest GitHub tree and an expected-head update, preserving unrelated files and rejecting concurrent changes. The live Pages build and browser checks are performed after publication; local generation alone is not evidence that Pages has deployed.
