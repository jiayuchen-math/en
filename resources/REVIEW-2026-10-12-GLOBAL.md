# Worldwide researcher and Commonwealth / North America expansion

Review date: October 12, 2026. This is a dated source review, not live monitoring.

## Actual directory size

- 1,195 distinct researcher records across 18 country/territory tags.
- 869 explicitly published professional emails, each with a contact source and review date.
- 882 records with published topic evidence, 42 with departmental research-group membership only, and 271 with research topics not verified. These categories are disjoint.
- Three previously verified recent student-completion records are retained unchanged. No current individual student vacancy is asserted.
- 225 study/research routes: 49 undergraduate, 99 masters, 64 doctorates, four preparatory, seven postdoctoral and two visiting/research-institute routes.
- 25 new routes in the UK, US, Canada, Australia and New Zealand; three existing UBC/MIT degree records rechecked.
- The existing 400 works, 80 learning websites, notes and PDFs are retained. Their review dates are not changed to imply fresh content verification.

## Researcher evidence and limits

Current public department rosters were used for affiliation, with individual profiles for interests and contacts where accessible. Institutions expanded include Wisconsin–Madison, Oxford, UBC, Illinois, Northwestern, Cambridge, UNSW, Waterloo, Bath, Copenhagen, HKUST, MIT, Pennsylvania, Chicago, Tokyo, Lisbon Técnico, New Mexico, ETH and NUS. The original curated international records remain.

Names, public emails and unique personal-profile URLs were checked for duplicate identities. Shared department-page URLs were not used to merge different people. Explicit emeritus, retired, visiting, adjunct, student and postdoctoral entries were excluded from this potential-supervisor expansion. Source-listed ranks and dates can still lag actual appointments: publication of a profile does not prove tenure status, supervision rights or current openings.

Several institutional pages reject automated reading or return verification/JavaScript stubs. Such failures were not treated as faculty evidence. NUS was read through the accessible official web result; challenged Toronto and inaccessible substitute rosters were not guessed. Existing public HTTP author pages are retained as observed sources rather than silently inventing secure equivalents.

The directory covers mathematics and neighbouring mathematical sciences, including applied mathematics, statistics and mathematical physics. It is NOT a complete worldwide faculty census. Outside the main expanded rosters some countries have only a few curated people. Countries/institutions without entries are not evidence of no suitable supervisors.

Public contact information is provided for individual academic enquiries, not automated email campaigns. No emails were sent. Group membership is explicitly distinguished from personal research interests. Missing addresses and finer topics remain unknown.

## Admissions: meaning of numbers

Each new record links its actual requirement, cost, funding or timing evidence. Published minimum eligibility is not a competitive admitted-GPA estimate. No inferred admission odds, school-name “safe” classification or fabricated cohort is supplied.

- UBC's [current admissions page](https://www.math.ubc.ca/graduate/prospective-students/admissions) reports 30–40 new students from over 300 applications, combining MSc and PhD, not separate quotas. Its next stated intake is September 2027; application deadline December 23, 2026 and documents December 31, 2026. Qualification-specific Canadian/US minima are not universal conversions.
- Wisconsin's [department FAQ](https://www.math.wisc.edu/graduate/faq/) reports a typical intended entering class of 20–28, depending on need/funding, without a year-labelled future quota. It distinguishes the independently admitted MA-FAS from doctoral funding and the embedded MA-FR. No average admitted GPA is supplied.
- [Leeds MSc 2027](https://courses.leeds.ac.uk/f669/mathematics-msc) gives total UK/international fees and different deadlines. The lower 2:2 threshold on [Liverpool MSc 2027](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/courses/mathematical-sciences-msc/2027) is not a promise of admission.
- Open University 2026/27 prices, UNSW 2026 indicative fees and Liverpool's 2026/27 Bracken award are explicitly historical relative to October/September 2027. Module pre-booking is not completed admission/registration.
- Saskatchewan and Memorial summaries print funding amounts without sufficiently explicit award periods in those summaries. The directory does not assume annual cash, unconditional receipt, or a renewed 2027 award. Linked policies and written offers govern.
- Manitoba's MSc table separates Canadian **and US** applicants from other international applicants; MSc, PhD and pre-master's schedules are not conflated. Its dynamic PhD deadline table was not fully accessible, so MSc deadlines were not copied over.
- [MIT mathematics admissions](https://math.mit.edu/academics/grad/phd/admission/index.html) and [Northwestern](https://www.math.northwestern.edu/graduate/application.html) have different GRE policies. Older TOEFL-format paragraphs and expired fee waivers are not treated as current universal rules.

Many departments do not publish mathematics-specific incoming places or admitted-grade distributions in the reviewed notices. These fields remain explicitly unverified. This batch does not claim complete numeric funding/tuition/cohort data for all 225 routes. A student visa alone does not establish home tuition, scholarship eligibility or immigration eligibility.

## Verification and publication scope

Search, combined email/topic-evidence filters, reset, 25-entry pagination, accent/alias handling, application-date uncertainties, local background assessment and internal links are tested. All complete entries remain in HTML for no-JavaScript access; external web links retain the original site tab. Existing note bodies and PDFs are excluded from the publication patch.

Structural checks and existing catalogue-preservation checks pass. The latest GitHub tree is compared before publication; a conditional non-forced main-branch update protects intervening changes. Publication and Pages deployment are checked separately, rather than assuming a successful commit means the live site is updated.
